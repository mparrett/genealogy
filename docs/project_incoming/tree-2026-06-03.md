Both Patricks (1829 James-son + 1831 Philip-son) were baptized **on the same day of the year, March 20** — two years apart, same

1. **Family tradition** — March 19 is St. Joseph's Day (the patron of the Universal Church and a popular baptismal date in Catholic

2. **Naming coincidence** — both children "Patrick" as a Patrick = St. Patrick = the Irish national patron. Catholic families across 
Ireland baptized boys "Patrick" frequently around March (closer to St. Patrick's Day, March 17).
3. **Indexing collision** — unlikely since parents differ.
