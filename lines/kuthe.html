Kuthe / Bridgefarmer Line

Germany → Missouri → Oregon → Arizona

The Kuthe line begins in Hesse, Germany, emigrating to Missouri in the 1830s. Frederick Kuthe established the family in America, and subsequent generations moved westward through Oregon before settling in Arizona.
