## 1. Per-document summary

### A. `higgins-dna-wicklow.md` (Mar 2022, ~830 lines, 8 images)
Mary Anne's master research paper on **the parents of Christopher Higgins (1803) and Bridget Darcy**. She argues — using Tony Pearson's tree, DNA matches, parish baptisms, Tithe Applotments, Griffith's Valuation, and tombstone transcriptions — that Christopher (1803) is the son of **Nicholas Higgins (1770–~1867) and Eleanora Smyth (1769–1811)**, and that Nicholas is the son of **John Higgins (1718–1808) and Anne Thomas (1733–1773)** of Baltyboys, Co. Wicklow. Identifies three Wicklow townlands of origin (Carrigacurra, Carrig, Ballynatona) and names ~10 DNA matches anchoring the connection. Rejects two alternate-parentage theories (Patrick Higgins + Mary Johnstone of Kilcock; James Higgins b. 1767).

### B. `higgins-research-mary.md` (Jan 2025, 45 lines)
Cover email accompanying the Wicklow doc. Names her **two highest-confidence DNA matches** to our line: (1) Bernadette McGuire (her 2nd cousin via Thomas 1900), (2) **Patricia Bri** — descended from Laurence + Mary Knight → Theresa Cecilia O'Reilly → Martha Eleanor O'Reilly Sparks → Patricia Cecelia Sparks Briggs (b. 1946). Notes other unattributed matches possibly from Conlan or Darcy lines. Offers DNA-sharing help and asks Matt about an Anne Higgins (b. 1878, Mary Verner's daughter) — fate unknown.

### C. `irish-times-article-maryanne.md` (Nov 2024, 215 lines)
Email thread reacting to an Irish Times "Five Points / Irish slums" article Matt sent. Mary Anne's response is mostly thematic (Penal Laws, Catholic discrimination, intergenerational trauma, why her grandfather inspected potatoes). The genealogically useful nugget is buried in earlier replies in the same thread: **her cousin theory** for the 1887 wedding witness (Christopher 1856 of Jersey City), her cousin chart, and her confirmation that **Anne Higgins (b. 1878) is missing from her records** — possible orphaning/adoption/death.

### D. `mary-knight-1930-death.md` (Oct–Nov 2024, 241 lines)
Email thread where Mary Anne announces finding Mary Knight Higgins's death record in the NYC database (Oct 30 2024) and discusses the Lawrence Higgins infant death record (12 Dec 1890, son of Laurence + Jane Knight, 12 Clarkson St., father's occupation **"L shore" = Longshoreman**). Most of the substance is Mary Anne's **cousin-theory walkthrough** for the 1887 witness (Christopher 1856 = son of Thomas 1833 of Jersey City), plus her reading of Mary Knight's death cert: parents **James Knight and Bridget Larney**, with "Larney" called out as a rare surname (per John Grenham's Irish Ancestors site) found only in small Irish pockets — *not* Sligo. Notes a separate Theresa-line descendant (Brittany Bepler) lists Mary Knight's origin as **Co. Sligo, surname "Courtney"** — a conflict.
