## Notes carried forward

**On the naturalization search (thread 1).** An Ancestry category-40 search (`James Gleeson`, b. 1863 Ireland, res. Wisconsin) returned **passenger arrivals only** — Queenstown→NY 1909 and 1910, Liverpool→NY 1880, and a 1955 Ontario crossing. **No naturalization or declaration records surfaced.** The 1880 Liverpool arrival is chronologically interesting against an assumed ~1883 emigration but is unverified and Liverpool departure is a poor fit for a Croom man. Next attempt should target the named collections directly rather than the category, and consider that Wisconsin naturalizations are often held **county-level** rather than federally.

**⚠️ Standing methodological rule, earned THREE times in one day.** A bare *"James Gleeson, farmer"* — or any bare forename + occupation — is close to worthless as an identifier in this project. **Three separate conclusions have been overturned by another man of the same name: James Gleeson of Hospital vs of Croom; Timothy senior vs junior; James Molony of Croagh vs James Moloney of Ballymackeamore.** Do not upgrade an identification on a naming-convention prediction plus a plausible age — get the father's name. Require a townland, a spouse, or a date before treating a name match as evidence.

**⚠️ On the "from Tipperary" tradition.** It is **one hedged, third-hand DM** whose only quoted word is **Cork**, from a correspondent who also supplied two claims since disproven. The 1833 Ballinvreena residence is a solid fact on its own; **do not let the two corroborate each other.** See the provenance warning in `research/findings/patrick-gleeson/facts.md`.
