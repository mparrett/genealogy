## Birth & Early Life

**Full Name:** Jessie Velma Kuthe
**Birth Date:** April 10, 1905
**Birth Place:** West Albany, Linn County, Oregon
**Source:** Oregon, State Births, 1842-1917; 1910 U.S. Census

**Parents:**
- **Father:** George Washington Kuthe (1869-1925)
- **Mother:** Huldah Imogene Bridgefarmer (1873-1907) — died Aug 6, 1907, when Jessie was 2

**Siblings (Kuthe):** Charles Austin (1897-1967), Frances Anita (1898-1926), Wallace/William Wallace (1901-1928), **Doris Pauline (1902-1996)** [[doris-kuthe]], George Leslie (1906-1962). Half-siblings via father's later family: Neva, Eleanor, an infant (1912).

**Migration:** The family moved from **Albany, Oregon to Phoenix, Arizona c. 1911-1912** (by 1912 a half-sibling was born in Phoenix). **This train journey is the subject of Jessie's journal** (see below).

## Writing

- **Journal:** first-person account of the **family's train trip from Oregon to Arizona** (~1911-12). Original held by nephew **Howard**; a copy is held by grandson **Robin Rosser** (currently misplaced as of 2025). **Not published online.**
- **Ford Times, March 1959:** her essay portraying **Albany, Oregon as "a transplanted New England village"** (her girlhood home) — *distinct from the journal*. Matt owns a purchased copy. [CONFIRMED issue via the 1959 newspaper write-up below.]
- **"Jessie Kuthe Adair Tells Of Albany's Past"** — Albany Democrat-Herald, **Mar 21, 1959, p.19** — a local write-up **about her Ford Times piece** (not the piece itself). Notes she lived in Albany as a girl and is "the descendant of pioneers who came to the Willamette valley by covered wagon."
