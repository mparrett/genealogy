## Uncertain / Needs Verification

### Frank Hambrock = Frank Preston Mowrey = "Fred Mowrey"
- **All three are the same person.** Ancestry has duplicate entries.
- Born Dec 1897, Texas (NOT Ohio — Ancestry birthplace is wrong)
- Listed in 1900 census as "Frank Hambrock," age 2, in Samuel Mowry's household
- Raised as Frank Preston Mowrey after Effie married Samuel
- Known as "Fred Mowrey" later in life
- Died 6 Jun 1980, Gila Bend, AZ. Buried at Pioneer Cemetery, Gila Bend (as "Frank Preston Mowery," rank: Private, US Army)
- Effie's obituary: "Fred Mowrey, of Gila Bend" — confirmed same person via location match
- WWII draft card (as Frank Preston Mowrey) lists mother as "Mrs. A.M. Dawson"
- Ancestry's "Fred Mowrey (c. 1905–1958)" is likely yet another duplicate with wrong dates

### 1900 Census — All Children Listed as Hambrock
- **Source:** 1900 U.S. Federal Census, Justice Precinct 1, Lamar, Texas (Sheet 18, Dwelling 352)
- Household: Samuel S Mowry (33), Effie Mowry (22), Cecil Hambrock (5), Frank Hambrock (2), Rob Hambrock (5/12)
- All three children carry the **Hambrock** surname, not Mowrey
- "Rob Hambrock" age 5 months = **Raymond Robert Mowrey** (b. Dec 1899)
- Name change to Mowrey came sometime after 1900
- **Frank was conceived c. March 1897, before the June 1897 marriage to Samuel** — biologically a Hambrock child, not a Mowrey
- Joseph Hambrock (1876–1900) was likely still alive at Frank's conception, though Effie married Samuel in June 1897 — the timeline is ambiguous

### Mrs. P. H. Reed
- Confirmed as a real daughter — Effie's obituary lists "Mrs. P. H. Reed, of Colusa, Calif."
- Likely the "P. H. Reed (b. c. 1900)" in Ancestry. "Ms Phil Freed" is probably a garbled duplicate of this same person.

### Other Uncertain Children
- "Coker Armack" (b. c. 1896) — still unclear, may be misattribution.

### KEY RESEARCH QUESTION: Raymond's Paternity
- Raymond Robert Mowrey (b. Dec 1899) is recorded as "Rob Hambrock" in the 1900 census, living in Samuel Mowrey's household at 5 months old
- All three children in the household carry the Hambrock surname — not Mowrey
- Two scenarios:
  1. **Recording artifact:** The census taker used the Hambrock surname for all the children because the older two were Hambrocks, and the household was a blended family. Raymond is biologically Samuel's son.
  2. **Raymond is biologically Joseph Hambrock's son.** Joseph was alive until c. 1900 and could have been in contact with Effie during the Mowrey marriage. The Hambrock surname on a 5-month-old in his stepfather's household would be unusual unless there was a reason.
- No DNA evidence or other records currently available to resolve this.
- Both scenarios are modeled as separate entries in the Ancestry tree (Frank Hambrock + Frank Preston Mowrey; Rob Hambrock + Raymond Robert Mowrey) pending resolution.
- Note: Ancestry lists Ohio as birthplace for the Hambrock entries, but the 1900 census records Texas — Ohio birthplace is likely wrong.
