# Andrew Montgomery Dawson - Research Findings

## Confirmed Facts

### Birth
- 9 March 1879, Hailsboro, Texas
- **Source:** Ancestry profile; Social Security records

### Parents
- Father: Andrew Dawson (1855–)
- Mother: Ann Riley (1855–)
- **Source:** Ancestry profile

### Marriage to Effie May Coker
- Date: 9 August 1911, Sebastian County, Arkansas
- Spouse: Effie May Coker (1878–1958)
- Children: Loyce Maxine Dawson (1913–1983), Thelma Dawson Armack (1915–1989)
- Also listed as stepfather to Effie's earlier children
- **Source:** Arkansas marriage records; Sebastian County records

### Daughter from Unknown Relationship
- Ms Phil Freed (b. c. 1900) — listed in Ancestry as Andrew's daughter; unclear whether this is from a prior relationship or a misattribution

### Occupations
- 1917–1918: Coconino County, AZ (WWI draft registration)
- 1920: Hostler, Williams, AZ (head of household)
- 1930: Head electrician, Williams, AZ
- **Source:** U.S. Federal Census 1920, 1930; WWI draft registrations

### Residences
- 1908: Winslow, Navajo County, AZ
- 1911: Fort Smith, Sebastian County, AR (at time of marriage)
- 1914: Winslow, AZ
- 1917–1918: Coconino County, AZ
- 1920–1936: Williams, Coconino County, AZ
- **Source:** Census records; voter registrations; city directories

### Death
- 12 February 1938, Winslow, Navajo County, Arizona
- Buried in Winslow, Navajo County
- **Source:** Arizona death records; Find a Grave; Social Security records

### Identified as "Mr. Dawson" / "A. M. Dawson"
- Previously known in Mowrey research only as "Mr. Dawson" — Effie's second husband after Samuel
- 1928 Williams News: Raymond Mowrey described as "son of Mrs. A. M. Dawson of this city"
- **Source:** Williams News, Sep 7, 1928
