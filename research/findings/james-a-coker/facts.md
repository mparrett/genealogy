## Speculative / Needs Verification

### Civil War Service - U.S. Colored Troops — LIKELY WRONG PERSON
- Ancestry records list military service in "U.S. Colored Troops" with additional entry for "Georgia"
- Sources cited: U.S. Civil War Soldier Records, U.S. Union Soldiers Compiled Service Records, Civil War Pension Index, Freedmen's Bureau Records, Civil War Prisoner of War Records
- **However, the 1880 census (Precinct 5, Red River, TX) records J.A. Coker as White.** This strongly suggests the USCT records belong to a different James A. Coker.
- A 1900 census record (Brunswick, Glynn, Georgia) lists a "James A. Coker" — this is also a different person (our James died in 1891)
- **Almost certainly a case of Ancestry merging records from two or more different James A. Cokers.**
- **Source:** Ancestry profile; 1880 U.S. Federal Census; status SPECULATIVE — likely disproven

### Voter Registration 1867–1869
- Texas voter registration listed — James would have been 12–14, too young to vote. May be his father or another relative.
- **Source:** Texas, U.S., Voter Registration Lists, 1867-1869; status SPECULATIVE
