## Q1 — Is "Patritio Gleeson" a unique man in the Croom register? (highest priority)

The reciprocal-witness argument for Catherine's 1879 marriage (Patrick witnessed the Shanahans' wedding two months earlier; the Shanahans then witnessed Catherine's) assumes there was only one adult Patrick Gleeson transacting business in Croom parish at the time. That assumption hasn't been tested, and the same marriage register has at least one unrelated Gleeson marriage in the same window (*Gulielmum Gleeson et Catherinam Feorey*, Oct 1880), confirming the surname wasn't rare there.

**Steps:**
- Sweep the full Croom marriage register (not just p.161) for every appearance of a Patrick Gleeson, as principal or witness, 1870–1885.
- Cross-check against Griffith's Valuation for Croom-area townlands for more than one Patrick Gleeson holding land/property in the period.
- Resolve against the existing Baggotstown West lead (`research/analysis/` — Gleeson two-brothers-at-Baggotstown-West finding, c.1850) — confirm whether that's a different generation, or someone who could still be active in 1879.

**If a second Patrick Gleeson turns up as plausibly active in 1879:** downgrade the witness-reciprocity argument from "confirms" to "consistent with."

## Q2 — Identify the "Bridget Gleeson" godmother at Catherine's 1862 baptism

`facts.md` currently treats her as an unidentified Gleeson kinswoman, "possibly his sister." But `research/results/gleeson-catherine-hayes-search.md` already documents a Bridget Gleeson who married Thomas Shea at Croom on 27 Aug 1864, separately hypothesized as Patrick's sister.

**Steps:**
- Check whether the 1862 godmother and the 1864 bride are the same Bridget Gleeson.
- If yes: this is a second data point on an existing hypothesis, not a fresh loose end — correct the "unidentified kinswoman" framing in `facts.md`.
- If no: treat as a genuinely new open lead, and don't let it sit inside the confidence rating of the baptism record itself.

## Q3 — Pin down Nora's real birth year (lower priority, already an open task)

Not new work — this is the existing open item (Croom civil birth register, 1875–1881). Nora's cert gives 10 Jan 1876; Catherine's cert (5 years later, different informant memory) gives 14 Feb 1876 — same year, different day/month, most likely explained by informant Helen Ryan not actually knowing either woman's real birth year rather than any data cross-contamination. Resolving Nora's real birth year independently removes the coincidence as a live question mark, though it doesn't touch the core identification either way.

**Steps:**
- Search irishgenealogy.ie Croom civil births 1875–1881 for a Patrick + Margaret Gleeson child not already accounted for.
- Check whether Margaret's 29 Sep 1875 baptism (already documented) was a twin entry.
