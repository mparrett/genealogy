# Howard's Foster Chapter: St. Mary's Academy, Silver City, NM

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Subject:** Where Howard Robert Higgins (later Birch) and his cousin Willis "Bill" Rosser spent the early-mid 1930s while their mothers (the Kuthe sisters) established themselves.

## The chapter in brief

During the Great Depression, the two Kuthe sisters' young sons were boarded together at a Catholic institution in **Silver City, New Mexico** run by nuns, while their mothers built their lives nearby:

- **Willis "Bill" Andrew Rosser** — b. Dec 1925, Phoenix; son of **Jessie** Velma Kuthe (Rosser).
- **Howard Robert Higgins** — b. 15 Sep 1927, Tucson; son of **Doris** Pauline Kuthe (Higgins). [birth cert confirmed]
- **Estimated period: c. 1930–1934.** Family lore: nuns ran the place; the boys "complained of being hungry a lot," but lived and went to school there.
- The cousins were reportedly **sent in together**. **Jessie later removed Bill**; **Howard remained**, then went to **Robert & Sarah Birch** in **Agua Caliente, AZ**, taking the Birch surname.
- **Date anchors (both ends now firm):**
  - **1930 census (Apr 1930):** Howard (age 2) and his mother **Doris** were lodging with the **Parkman family in Buckeye, Maricopa, AZ** — *not* yet in Silver City. (Household: Isaac H. & Mary A. Parkman; Maizer/Elsie King; Elsie, Merle, Carroll Parkman; Doris Higgins 27; Howard Higgins 2.)
  - **1940 census (back-ref Apr 1935):** Howard at **"Agua Caliente, Maricopa, AZ"** with the Birches (see howard-higgins/1940-census.md).
  - ⇒ **St. Mary's window narrows to ~1931–1934** (after Buckeye, before Agua Caliente). The **1931 Doris-and-Howard resort photo** (agua-caliente.md) shows the Birch tie may have started early.
- **The Parkman caregiver network:** the Parkmans appear in Howard's life on **both** ends — Buckeye 1930 and again in 1940 Phoenix, where **Elsie Parkman** is in his household. Worth tracing as a parallel to the Birches.

The mothers' whereabouts fit: **Jessie was single in Silver City, NM in the 1930 census** (903 W. 12th St., working for the Forest/Power office, "near campus" — near the New Mexico teachers' college) and her tree shows her there again in **1935**. **Doris** was pursuing her education/nursing (she was lodging in Buckeye, AZ in 1930).

## The institution — identified

**St. Mary Magdalene's Academy** (a.k.a. **St. Mary's Academy**), Silver City, Grant County, NM.

- Catholic **boarding school, grades 1–12**, on **Alabama Street**, NW outskirts of Silver City.
- Operated by the **Sisters of St. Joseph** (local community: "Sisters of St. Joseph of Silver City").
- Site acquired 1918 (former Lee/Jackson properties); a **fire in December 1922** prompted a building campaign.
- Now a **National Register Historic District**; the academy **closed 40+ years ago**.
- Sources: Henry C. Trost Historical Org., "St. Mary Magdalene's Academy" (henrytrost.org); Susan Berry & Sharman Apt Russell, *Built to Last: An Architectural History of Silver City, New Mexico* (Silver City Museum Society, 1986), pp. 66, 105.

**The researcher who identified it:** **Susan M. Berry** (sberry@zianet.com) — a Silver City historian and **co-author of the book above** (i.e., the local authority). Engaged in 2025 via **Sharyn "Sharon" Kuthe** (moleson2001@yahoo.com / sharonkuthe@gmail.com), who forwarded findings to Matt and Frances Rosser Taylor.

### What Susan Berry found / didn't find
- Confirmed **Willis (Bill) was lodged at St. Mary's**; it was "the only local institution involving nuns."
- The **1930 US Census for Silver City listed only the nuns** at St. Mary's, **not the students** — so it does not directly place either boy there.
- Searched the **Silver City Enterprise** and **Silver City Independent** (weeklies); clippings 1930–1935 attached; **no 1933 articles naming Jessie**.
- Found **nothing on Robert & Sarah Birch** and **nothing on Higgins** in those papers.
