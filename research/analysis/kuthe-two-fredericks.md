# Disambiguation: Two Frederick Kuthes

Early research conflated two separate Kuthe families who emigrated from Hesse, Germany in the 1830s–1840s. A 1834 Baltimore ship manifest resolves the confusion.

## Our Frederick (Andreas's son)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Father** | Andreas Kuthe (b. c. 1798) |
| **Mother** | Anna D. Kuthe (b. c. 1800) |
| **Origin** | Near Darmstadt, Hesse |
| **Arrival** | April–June 1834, Baltimore, MD |
| **Age at arrival** | 7–10 (child) |
| **Sibling on manifest** | Margareth (age 3) |
| **Full name** | George Frederick Kuthe |
| **Marriage** | Sarah Ann Patterson (née Steagall), 19 Jan 1868, Dent Co., MO |
| **Son** | George Washington Kuthe (1869–1925), our direct ancestor |
| **Death** | 10 Feb 1900, Dent County, MO |

## Other Frederick (Heinrich's son)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Father** | Heinrich Küthe (b. c. 1793) |
| **Mother** | Anna Küthe |
| **Origin** | Vöhl, Waldeck, Hesse |
| **Arrival** | 13 June 1845, New Orleans (ship *Olbers*, from Bremen) |

## Why They Were Confused

1. Both named Frederick/Friederich Kuthe
2. Both from Hesse, Germany
3. Both arrived in the 1830s–1840s
4. Both had a sister named Margareth/Margaretha
5. "Darmstadt" (family sources) was conflated with "Vöhl" (Olbers manifest) — both are in Hesse but ~150 km apart
6. The Mary C. Kuthe who married Louis Schleicher ("born in Germany in 1824, came to America in 1845") was assumed to be our Frederick's sister, but she traveled on the Olbers with Heinrich's family

## How They Were Distinguished

- The 1834 Baltimore manifest lists **Andreas** Kuth (age 36) with son **Fredk** — matching the family tradition of father Andreas (b. c. 1798) exactly
- The 1845 Olbers manifest lists **Heinrich** Küthe (age 52) with son **Friederich** — a different father entirely
- Our Frederick's marriage record (1868) uses the name "KUTHE, GEORGE F." — he was George Frederick, arriving as a child, not the 18-year-old Friederich on the Olbers
- The Olbers family came from **Vöhl, Waldeck**; our family came from near **Darmstadt** — different parts of Hesse

## Possible Kinship

Despite being separate families, Andreas and Heinrich may be related. The overlapping names across both manifests (Anna as wife, Friedrich and Margareth as children) are suggestive of extended kin from a common origin. If so, Vöhl could still be our family's origin — but this remains unconfirmed.

## Impact on Existing Research

- The 1845 Olbers immigration story in Frederick's bio has been corrected to 1834 Baltimore
- Mary C. Kuthe is no longer considered a confirmed sibling of our Frederick (but could still be a relative)
- Exact origin within Hesse is uncertain — Darmstadt from family tradition, Vöhl from the possibly related Olbers family; neither confirmed by primary source
- Andreas Kuthe is now confirmed by primary source (previously unverified family tradition)
