# Disambiguation: Two Frederick Kuthes

Early research conflated two separate families who emigrated from Hesse, Germany. Both had a son named Friedrich, leading to decades of confusion.

**April 2026 update:** A Baltimore 1834 passenger record previously used to anchor "our" Frederick was found to belong to a family named **Huth**, not Kuth/Kuthe. See research/analysis/kuthe-baltimore-1834.md. The Kuthe line now has no confirmed immigration record.

This family was erroneously attributed to the Kuthe line due to the "Kuth" misreading and the coincidental match of father "Andreas" with a son "Friedrich" of the right age.

## Why They Were All Confused

1. Three families with sons named Frederick/Friedrich from Hesse
2. The Huth quarterly abstract was misread as "Kuth" and matched to the Kuthe line
3. "Andreas Kuth" with son "Fredk" (age 7) appeared to confirm Kuthe family tradition perfectly
4. The Olbers manifest had another Friedrich Küthe from a third family
5. "Darmstadt" (1870 census) was conflated with "Vöhl" (Olbers manifest) — both in Hesse but ~150 km apart
6. The Mary C. Kuthe who married Louis Schleicher ("born in Germany in 1824, came to America in 1845") was assumed to be our Frederick's sister, but she traveled on the Olbers with Heinrich's family

## How They Are Distinguished

- **Huth ≠ Küthe ≠ Kuthe** — three separate surnames, three separate families
- The Huth family is confirmed by BRG55 index cards under that spelling
- The Küthe family is documented on the 1845 Olbers manifest from Vöhl
- Our Kuthe family is known only from Missouri records (1860s onward); no immigration record has been found
- Our Frederick's marriage record (1868) uses "KUTHE, GEORGE F." — he was George Frederick

## Possible Kinship

The overlapping given names (Andreas/Anna/Friedrich/Margareth) across the Huth and Kuthe families are suggestive but may be coincidental — these were extremely common German names. Any connection between the families is unproven.

## Impact on Existing Research

- The 1834 Baltimore record belongs to the **Huth family**, not the Kuthe line
- The 1845 Olbers record belongs to the **Küthe family** from Vöhl
- Our Frederick Kuthe has **no confirmed immigration record**
- Father Andreas is family tradition only — no longer supported by any primary source
- Mother's name and sibling Margareth are unconfirmed
- Mary C. Kuthe is not a confirmed relative
- Exact origin within Hesse is uncertain — "Darmstadt" from census is all we have
