# Baltimore 1834 Record: Huth, not Kuthe

**Date of finding:** 2026-04-13

## Summary

A quarterly abstract of Baltimore passenger arrivals (NARA M596, Roll 4, p. 67) listing an "Andreas Kuth" family was long attributed to the Kuthe line. Research into the Baltimore passenger index cards (BRG55) in April 2026 confirmed the family's surname is **Huth** — a different family entirely. The Kuthe line has no confirmed immigration record.

## The Huth family (BRG55 index cards)

Two passenger cards and two remarks/verifications cards survive:

**Card 2596-1-19 — HUTH, Anna Doroth**
- Age 34, Female, Farmer
- Last permanent residence: **Weilhausen**
- Ship: **Julius Eduard**
- Date: **June 14, 1834**
- Destination: **Pittsburg** [Pittsburgh]
- Remarks card lists: Andreas (36), Friedrich (7), Margareth (3), Anna Margareth (60)

**Card 2596-1-22 — HUTH, Anna Margareth**
- Age 60, Female, Farmer
- Last permanent residence: **Weilhausen**
- Ship: **Julius Eduard**
- Date: **June 14, 1834**
- Destination: **Pittsburg** [Pittsburgh]
- Remarks card lists: Andreas (36), Anna-Doroth (34), Friedrich (7), Margareth (3)

**Reconstructed Huth family:**
- Andreas Huth (36) — head of household, farmer
- Anna Dorothea (34) — wife
- Friedrich (7) — son
- Margareth (3) — daughter
- Anna Margareth (60) — likely Andreas's mother

This is **not** Frederick Kuthe's family. The coincidence of names (Andreas, Friedrich, Margareth) and ages led to the misattribution, compounded by the quarterly abstract being misread as "Kuth" instead of "Huth."

## Why the error persisted

- The quarterly abstract handwriting was read as "Kuth" — close enough to "Kuthe" to seem like a match
- Ancestry.com indexed the family under "Kuth," making it discoverable in Kuthe surname searches
- The father "Andreas" matched Kuthe family tradition
- The son "Fredk" age 7 matched Frederick Kuthe's estimated birth year
- Searching BRG55 for "Kuthe" returned nothing, which was attributed to indexing gaps rather than a wrong surname

## What this means for the Kuthe line

The 1834 Baltimore record was treated as the primary anchor for:
- Frederick's arrival in the US
- The identification of Andreas Kuthe as his father
- The mother's name "Anna D."
- The sibling Margareth
- Distinguishing "our" Frederick from the Vöhl/Olbers Frederick

**All of these claims now revert to unconfirmed.** What remains:

- Frederick Kuthe lived in Dent County, MO (census, tax, marriage, probate records — all solid)
- He was born in Germany (1870 census: "Darmstadt"; enlistment: "Germany")
- He married Sarah Ann Patterson in 1868 (Dent Co. marriage register: "KUTHE, GEORGE F.")
- Father named Andreas per family tradition only — no primary source
- Birth year c. 1827 still supported by 1870 census (age 43) and 1865 enlistment (age 36)
- **No confirmed immigration record exists for the Kuthe family**

## Next steps

- Look for Andreas Kuthe in Missouri records independent of the Baltimore manifest
- Consider German church records from the Darmstadt area
