## 2. Confirmed family structure

**Jessie Velma Kuthe**, b. 10 Apr 1905 West Albany, Oregon; d. 27 Mar 1993 Nogales, Santa Cruz Co., Arizona. Daughter of George Washington Kuthe (1869–1925) and Huldah Imogene Bridgefarmer (1873–1907).

**Marriage 1 — Willis Andrew Rosser Sr. (1898–1969):** m. 5 Jun 1922, Phoenix, AZ (3 sources incl. Arizona County Marriage Records). Divorced by April 1940.
- Son: **Willis Andrew "Bill" Rosser Jr.** (25 Dec 1923 Phoenix – 5 Mar 1999, Tucson/Sonoran desert).

**Marriage 2 — Roy William Adair (1889–1969):** m. **~1940/41, San Francisco — RECORD NOT YET FOUND** (see §4). Roy d. 23 Jan 1969, Los Angeles.
- Daughter: **Imogene Anne "Juby" Adair** (3 May 1941 Los Angeles – 25 May 2021 Nogales), later **Imogene Bell**.

**Sister in the household:** **Doris Pauline Kuthe (McGuire)** (1902–1996). Juby's 2021 obituary: she grew up on Court Street, Nogales "with her mother, Jessie Adair and aunt, Doris McGuire." Matches the 1950 census, where Jessie is listed as "Sister" / "Relation to Head of House" — i.e., living in Doris's household.

### Grandchildren / great-grandchildren (Jessie's descendants)

- Via **Bill Rosser** + wife Ivy Elizabeth (married ~50 yrs): Robin W. Rosser, Rosemary A. Rosser, Frances Rosser Taylor. Great-grandchildren incl. Dean Floerchinger, Joelle, Nathan, Clint Rosser.
- Via **Juby (Imogene) Bell** + husband George Graham Bell: Daniel Graham Bell (wife Roxanne), Jessica Bell Hinderstein (husband William). Five great-great-grandchildren: Danielle, Matthew, Aidan Bell; Sarah & John Hinderstein.

The Adair marriage is the one genuine husband mystery. Free-text newspaper search ("Roy Adair" + Jessie; "Jessie Rosser/Adair") is too noisy to isolate her. Better targets: **California county marriage indexes** (San Francisco / Los Angeles, 1940–41), the **Newspapers.com Marriage Index** filtered to CA, and the **1942 SF city directory** transition ("Mrs. Jessie Adair, 3329 25th Ave").

### The "Vickers" name — RESOLVED (phantom, deleted)

The uploaded Ancestry dump listed "Jessie Vickers" as an alternate name, but it did not correspond to any retained sourced record. **Matt has since deleted it from the tree.** Conclusion: a misindexed/misattributed hint for a different person — **not a third husband.** Closed.

### Note: keep Bill Rosser's marriage separate from Jessie's

Per Sharon (2025), **Bill Rosser** (Jessie's son) married **Ivy Elizabeth** in **California in early 1943, during a WWII furlough**; Ivy came from **England** with their daughter. This is *Bill's* marriage, not Jessie's — flagged here only to avoid conflating it with Jessie's husband timeline.
