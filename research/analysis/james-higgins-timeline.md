## Where the gaps are (ranked)

1. **~1919–1926 — the westward career years.** The single biggest hole: no record yet placing him between WWI and his 1926 Arizona marriage. This is where the story of *how* he became a western civil engineer (and crossed paths with Doris) lives.
2. **1892–~1917 — origins, family, and training.** Parents unknown; no childhood census; where he studied engineering is unknown.
3. **1930 point-in-time**, and the **1930–38 SF specifics** (thin, but city directories should fill it easily).
4. **DOB conflict** (Feb 2 vs 20 Oct 1892) — a data-quality gap to resolve from the documents you hold.

## Fastest ways to fill them

- **Provide the draft card + death cert** → locks true DOB, parents, and his ~1917–18 residence/occupation. This also gives parents' names, which is the key to disambiguating him from the many other NY "James Higgins" of the same age.
- **Pull the census anchors: 1900, 1910, 1920, 1930.** 1920 and 1930 are the crucial missing ones. (Search by parents' names once known, not just "James Higgins.")
- **City directories** (whatever cities the draft card/1920 census point to, plus SF 1927–38) → year-by-year residence & occupation.
- **1926 Maricopa Co. marriage license** → his residence and occupation right before the SF move.

## Ruled out
- **1920 census, Lancaster Ward 8, PA (James Higgins, b.~1891 NY, Lodger):** NOT him — occupation **Weaver, Silk Mill**, **married**, both parents **Ireland**. A same-name, same-age, same-birthplace coincidence.
