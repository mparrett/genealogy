## B. Shaky attachments on the Ancestry page to VET (the "confusing places")

1. **1906 Spokane "transitman" ⚔ 1906 NYC Protectory.** Contradicts the project's best-documented fact — the **NY Catholic Protectory commitment of Aug 1906** (resident card #40130; committed for destitution; aunt Mrs. Quigley, 65 Downing St.; mother in Ward's Island asylum). A 14-year-old can't be in the Bronx Protectory *and* a transitman in Spokane. → **Spokane-1906 is almost certainly a different James Higgins; detach or mark speculative.**
2. **1910 San Francisco "lodger" ⚔ 1910 Bronx Protectory census.** Profile shows an SF 1910 lodger; our notes cite the 1910 federal census enumerating him at the Protectory (Bronx). Only one is him; the Protectory is far better anchored. → **SF-1910 lodger is suspect** (this is the 1910 record open in MyHeritage — resolve before trusting).
3. **First marriage to "? Higgins (1895–)".** Real signal ("Mrs. James E. Higgins," 1916 Spokane society note), but **wife unidentified** and **no record of how/when it ended** before the 1926 Doris marriage. → Needs the Spokane marriage record + her identity + a death/divorce pre-1926.
4. **Duplicate sister "Mary Higgins".** Profile has "Mary Higgins 88" AND "Mary A Higgins Hamm," both 1888–1913, plus half-sibling "Mary Higgins AltNY maybeHamm." → Likely one person entered 2–3×; **dedupe.**
5. **Birth-date spread / birth-index pile-up.** Keep **Feb 2 1892 (cert #6077)**. Aug 2 1892 = Protectory copy error; military "06/02/90" = wrong. Many NY birth-index sources hang on the profile — **prune strays** (the "20 Oct 1892 / cert #41717" flagged earlier is a *different* man).
