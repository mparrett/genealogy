# Gleeson × Moloney — NLI register & census sweep (2026-06-09)

**Scope:** Reading the NLI parish-register images and the 1911 census to (1) find the
Patrick Gleeson × Margaret Moloney marriage, (2) resolve whether Kate Moloney (Kelly)
is Margaret's sister or niece, and (3) locate Margaret's death. Executed from the
browser handoff brief of the same date.

**Bottom line:** All three primary records were located and read. The marriage was
found in the Croagh/Kilfinny register (18 Nov 1860) — but it names the bride **Maria
Moloney**, not Margarita, and includes a **consanguinity dispensation**. The census
resolves Kate as Margaret's **niece** (Kate's father Jeremiah Moloney lived in the
Kelly household). Margaret's **death** is confirmed (18 May 1922, Croom, daughter as
informant).

---

## 1. Marriage — FOUND

**Croagh/Kilfinny RC marriage register**, NLI parish 0880, microfilm 02420/06 =
`vtls000634972`, **page 14** (covers May 1859 – Nov 1860).

Verbatim entry (Latin):

> **Novr. 18 [1860] — Patritius Gleeson de Croom & Maria Moloney de Kilfenny in
> Matrimonio conjuncti sunt, habita dispensatione tam in Banniis quam in 3tio aut 4to
> gradu Consanguinitatis. Testes: Michaele Moloney & Joanne [Nash?].**

Translation: *"18 November [1860] — Patrick Gleeson of Croom and Mary Moloney of
Kilfinny were joined in matrimony, a dispensation having been obtained both in the
banns and in the third or fourth degree of consanguinity. Witnesses: Michael Moloney
and John [Nash?]."*

Field-by-field confidence:

| Field | Reading | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Date | 18 Nov 1860 (day possibly 11) | High on month/year, med-high on day |
| Groom | Patritius Gleeson, *de Croom* | High |
| Bride | **Maria** Moloney, *de Kilfenny* | High — letterforms match other "Maria" entries on the page |
| Dispensation | banns + 3rd/4th degree consanguinity | High |
| Witness 1 | Michaele Moloney (Michael Moloney) | High |
| Witness 2 | Joanne [Nash?] — partly cut off at page edge | Low |

**Key points / flags:**

- **Bride's forename is "Maria" (Mary), not "Margarita" (Margaret).** This is the only
  Patrick Gleeson marriage in the register across the full searched window (see §4); no
  separate "Patritius Gleeson × Margarita Moloney" entry exists. Groom (*of Croom*),
  surname (Moloney *of Kilfinny*), date window, and a **Moloney witness** all fit the
  target couple perfectly — only the forename diverges. Her 1922 death record (§3)
  calls her *Margaret*, so she was Margaret in later/civil life. Open question:
  baptismal vs. used name.
- **Consanguinity dispensation (3rd/4th degree):** Patrick and his bride were blood
  relatives. New constraint on the family reconstruction — the Gleesons and Moloneys
  were already intermarried/related before this marriage.
- **This register format records no fathers or ages** — only names, residences,
  witnesses, and the dispensation. The brief's expectation that the marriage record
  would "name both fathers and give both ages" does not hold for the 1860 Latin
  register, and civil marriage registration did not begin until 1864. So **no record
  will lock the natal family via a named father.** Corroboration rests on the witness
  Michael Moloney plus the consanguinity note.

**Conclusion:** Kate Kelly née Moloney's father, **Jeremiah Moloney (b. ~1845)**, lived
in the household. Margaret was born ~1836, so Jeremiah is of *her* generation (most
likely her brother). That makes **Kate her niece**, and Kate's daughter Ellen/Helen a
**first-cousin-once-removed** to Catherine Hayes — consistent with the loose "cousin"
on Catherine's 1944 estate. This revises the earlier (2026-06-08) working assumption
that Kate was Margaret's *sister*.

**Caveats:**

- Kate's stated age (32) is implausible against a 15-year-old daughter — almost
  certainly understated, or the elder children are from an earlier Kelly marriage. The
  Form A "years married" and "children born/living" columns would clarify, but the
  household-return PDF would not render in-browser; the NAI transcription is used here.
- "Ellis" (age 8, b. ~1903) is likely the Ellen expected at ~6 (b. 3 Mar 1905); the age
  doesn't match cleanly. Worth confirming against her own birth record.
- **Jeremiah Moloney is the only Moloney in Ballymackeamore in 1911** — the Moloney
  presence in the townland had narrowed to this one elderly man living with his
  daughter's family.

## 3. Death — FOUND

**Civil death register, Croom SRD, 1922**, irishgenealogy.ie record `cide-3320749`,
register image entry **No. 293**.

Verbatim (English register):

> **No. 293 — Eighteenth May 1922, Croom — Margaret Gleeson — F — Widow — 74 years —
> House Keeper — Fractured femur, Shock, Certified — Informant: B. Gleeson, Daughter,
> present at death, Croom — Registered Fifteenth July 1922 — Michl. Shanahan, Asst.
> Registrar.**

Match analysis:

- **Croom + family informant (her own daughter, "B. Gleeson") + widow** → meets the
  brief's acceptance bar.
- **Age 74 (→ b. ~1848) is understated.** A woman who married in 1860 cannot have been
  born in 1848; a birth ~1836 (≈ age 86) fits far better. Understated ages on elderly
  Irish death records are routine.
- The informant "**B. Gleeson, Daughter**" (Bridget?) ties the record firmly to the
  family. Names her *Margaret* (cf. the *Maria* of the 1860 marriage).

## 4. Register sweep — what else is on the Croagh marriage pages

Gleeson/Moloney/Ballymackeamore entries:

  female Gleeson marrying as bride; coram Maria O'Brien & Flora Reilly[?]). Confirms
  other Gleesons in Kilfinny.
- **p.11, 30 Jul 1858** — *Edmundus Coll & Johanna Glavin de Ballymackeamore* (coram
  Patritio Glavin & Honora Coll). Glavin, not Gleeson/Moloney — but the Ballymackeamore
  townland appears.
- **p.14, 18 Nov 1860** — the Gleeson × Moloney marriage (§1).
- Page 15 (Nov 1860 – Jan 1862) fully read: thirteen marriages, none Gleeson/Moloney.

Tasks 2 (Hospital register) and 3 (Croom shadowed p.151) from the brief were fallbacks
for *finding* the marriage; rendered moot once §1 was located. Croom p.151 covers
Aug 1861 – Feb 1862, the wrong window for a Nov 1860 marriage. **A Croom duplicate on
its own Nov 1860 page remains an open follow-up** (Patrick was "de Croom," so the
marriage may have been recorded in both parishes), though the era's format won't add
fathers or ages.

## 5. Follow-up (same day): Croom duplicate + Catherine's baptism

Both in the Croom register film `vtls000634977`, which holds **baptisms** (pp. 4–84)
and **marriages** (pp. 146–153) in one film.

**(a) No Croom marriage duplicate.** Croom marriage **page 149** (Mar–Nov 1860) was read
in full. The November 1860 Croom marriages are: Conway × Sheehan (10th),
[Nealon/Patritius] × O'Neill (17th), and [Carney] × Hogan (24th) — **no Gleeson ×
Moloney, no 18 Nov entry.** The marriage was recorded only in Croagh (the bride's
parish), as Catholic custom predicts. The Croom register's format here also names no
fathers, confirming that no surviving record of this marriage names the fathers.

**(b) Catherine's baptism resolves the name question toward "Margaret."** Croom baptism
**page 81**, first entry:

> **Janÿ 31 [1862] — Catharinam filiam Patritii Gleeson & Margtm [Margaritam] Moloñy
> de Scagh — [sponsors] Johanne Moloney & Brig. Gleeson.**

*"31 January [1862] — Catherine, daughter of Patrick Gleeson & Margaret Moloney of
Scagh; sponsors John Moloney & Bridget Gleeson."*

- **Mother named "Margaret" (Margaritam), not Maria** — read at high zoom and contrasted
  against a "Mariam" written in an adjacent entry on the same page (distinct forms).
- **"de Scagh"** matches Patrick's residence (Scagh townland, Croom) exactly — firmly
  ties this baptism to our couple.
- **Sponsor John Moloney** corroborates the mother's Moloney family again. Godmother
  **Bridget Gleeson** is *not* the daughter Bridget who informed the 1922 death — that
  Bridget wasn't born until 1881, while Catherine (1862) was the firstborn. So the 1862
  godmother is a **different, unidentified Bridget Gleeson** in Patrick's circle c.1862
  (a sister, sister-in-law, or other relative) — a small new lead, not a match to the
  death informant.

**(c) Two more children's baptisms — both "Margaret."**

- **James (Jacobus), p.87, "Die 23" Dec 1863:** *"Jacobus filius Patritii Gleeson et
  **Margarita Molony**"*; sponsors Gulielmo [Nolan?] & Margarita Daly. Mother = Margaret.
- **Honora (Honoria), p.93, 24 Feb 1866:** *"Honoria filia Patritii Gleeson et
  **Margarita Molony**; Spons. Thoma Shea et Ellena Moloney."* Mother = Margaret;
  godmother **Ellen Moloney** (a third Moloney godparent across the children — cf. John
  Moloney for Catherine). **Date image-verified:** entry is **Die 24** under the
  "In Mense Februarii" header, day-sequence Feb 2/8/10/14/18/22/23/24, with the
  "In Mense Martii" header following below — so 24 Feb 1866, not 10 Mar. The "10 Mar"
  in `nli-parish-register-pipeline.md` was a draft transcription error; **reconcile to
  24 Feb** across the pipeline doc, facts.md, and the timeline YAML.

**Net on Maria vs. Margaret — RESOLVED.** Three children's baptisms (Catherine 1862,
James 1863, Honora 1866) **all** name the mother **Margaret (Margarita)**, as do the
1911 census, 1918 deed, and 1922 death. Only the **1860 marriage** writes **Maria**.
The verdict is clear: she was **Margaret Moloney**, and the marriage "Maria" is a lone
scribal variant — not a second name carried through life. The brief's "Margaret"
identification is confirmed. (A father of the bride is still never named in any of
these — see open questions.)
