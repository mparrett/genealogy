**Riverside City Cemetery, Mondovi (#88821): "No matches found for Gleeson."** Not one.

Both cemeteries are real and both are in Mondovi — so the conflict was genuine, not a garbled name. It simply resolves against the local paper.

### ⚠️ What I got wrong, and why it matters more than the fact itself

Earlier today I wrote:

> *"**Prefer the Leader-Telegram.** It is the local paper, it was written *after* the funeral rather than before, and it distinguishes the church from the cemetery — which is exactly the distinction the distant St. Cloud paper would collapse."*

Every clause of that is a reasonable-sounding heuristic. **All of it was wrong.** The distant paper, written the day he died and before the funeral had happened, had it right; the local paper, writing a week later with the funeral behind it, printed the wrong cemetery.

**The real error was not the wrong guess — it was adjudicating a factual conflict by plausibility when a primary source would settle it in five minutes.** I had two sources disagreeing, reached for a rule of thumb about which *kind* of source to trust, and wrote the conclusion into three files. The burial register equivalent — Find A Grave with a photographed family plot — took one lookup.

**New standing rule: when two sources conflict on a checkable fact, check it. Do not rank the sources.**

### Consequences

- **Thread 18b is closed, and its premise inverted.** It said "look at Riverside first." Look at Sacred Heart; he is there.
- The repo's existing note that **Maria Ann Fitzgerald and her father James Fitzgerald are at Sacred Heart** is **confirmed**, not thrown into doubt as I suggested.
- ⭐ **The plot is a research asset in its own right.** Eight Gleesons in one cemetery, with dates, gives us a US-side family group to check the documented children against — and it is where the *missing* children (twelve born, we have nine or ten) would show up if they were buried with the family.
- ⭐ **"Helen Pearl *Jene* Gleeson"** — *Jene* is the surname in both obituaries' *"Mrs. John Jene, Eau Claire"*. Matt's Bear note flagged the spelling as uncertain. **It is real, it is spelled Jene, and it is now attached to a dated memorial.** Note she is *Helen Pearl*, b. 3 Aug 1897 — while the tree carries a daughter **Jane Agnes Gleeson, b. 20 Jun 1897**. Same year, different name and day. **Unresolved: one person recorded two ways, or two different women.**

## 2. The two 1911 crossings — BOTH EXCLUDED

Chased because 1911 is the year of the census recording his mother at Main Street, Croom. Neither is our man, and the reason is decisive.

| Record | Ship / date | Sheet heading | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| coll. 1518 / **16820972**, BT26/488/10 | **Cedric** (White Star), New York → Queenstown, **15 Dec 1911** | *"Names and Descriptions of **BRITISH** Passengers"* | ❌ **Not him** |
| coll. 1518 / **17759945**, BT26/487 | New York → Liverpool & Queenstown, **13 Oct 1911** | *"Names and Descriptions of **BRITISH** Passengers"* | ❌ **Not him** |

**The discriminator is the sheet itself.** BT26 separates *alien* from *British* passengers, and our James — a **naturalized US citizen** — travelled on the **ALIEN** sheet in December 1908, with nationality entered as **U.S.A.** A British-subject sheet is the wrong document for him by definition.

Corroborating detail:
- **15 Dec 1911:** *Gleeson, James* — **3rd class, Labourer** — travelling with a **Daniel Gleeson**, also 3rd class. Our James had no documented brother Daniel, was a **farmer**, and was 48.
- **13 Oct 1911:** *Gleeson, James* — **Labourer**, 3rd class.

⚠️ Note the form's limitation: these 1911 BT26 sheets record ages only as **statistical tallies by nationality group** (English/Welsh/Scotch/Irish, adults vs children), **not as individual ages**. So no age could confirm or exclude — the nationality sheet did the work.

**Method note, since it went right this time:** I checked what the form actually was before drawing a conclusion from it. That is the procedural fix written into memory after the BT26 address-column error, applied on the very next document.

**Standing so far: exactly ONE documented return to Ireland — out on the *Lucania* 16 Dec 1908, home on the *Lusitania* 14 Feb 1909.** Do not multiply the trips without a US-citizen sheet to prove them.
