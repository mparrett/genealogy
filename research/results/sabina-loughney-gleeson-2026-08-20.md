## ⚠️ Two corrections this forces

### 1. The obituary's marriage date is wrong — a **fifth** newspaper error

The *Leader-Telegram* of 2 Feb 1946 says *"In Jan. **1928**, Mr. Gleeson married Mrs. Bina O'Brien."* The marriage was **14 January 1929**, St. Louis County, Minnesota — two sources on the tree, including the Minnesota Official Marriage System.

Added to the error table in `james-gleeson-obituaries-1946-all-printings.md`: across three printings we now have **two ages, two grandchild counts, two cemeteries, a birth year off by one, a first-marriage year off by sixteen, and a second-marriage year off by one.** Standing rule 4 holds up well.

### 2. ⚠️ Bina is NOT in the Mondovi family plot

She died at Mondovi but is **buried at Superior, Douglas County** — with the Loughneys, presumably beside Robert O'Brien. This matters for thread 18b/18c: the eight-person Sacred Heart plot is **James's first family**, and Bina's absence from it is expected, not a gap.

### 3. ⚠️ Do not misread her "Spouse and children" block

Ancestry lists James's children by Maria Ann Fitzgerald — James Joseph Sr, John Frank, Joseph Patrick, Margaret Mary — under Bina, and even interleaves their births into her timeline with her age beside them ("Birth of son James Joseph 'John' Gleeson Sr — **Age 10**"). **These are step-relationships displayed as if biological.** Bina bore **one** child: Madeline Genevieve, by Robert O'Brien, in 1911. A future session skimming that page could easily miscount James's children.
