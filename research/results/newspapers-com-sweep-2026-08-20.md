## ⭐ The headline: the paper we need is not on Newspapers.com

**Buffalo County, Wisconsin has NO digitized newspaper on Newspapers.com.**

- A search for `Gleason`, location **Mondovi, Wisconsin**, **all dates**, returns: *"Your search for Gleason returned zero good matches."*
- **Buffalo County never appears** in the statewide "locations with matches" facet. Searching `Gleeson` across Wisconsin 1908–09 returns 413 matches spread across Brown (260), Racine (91), Milwaukee (20), Eau Claire and Kenosha — **and no Buffalo County at all.**

**That is the whole story.** He lived and farmed at **Mondovi, Buffalo County** from about 1892 to 1941. The local weekly that would have carried his marriage, his farm, his neighbours, and his 1908–09 voyage to Ireland is simply not in the database.

**The Mondovi Herald, 1890–1923, is the single most valuable un-consulted source in this whole line.** Held on **microfilm at the Wisconsin Historical Society**, which does lookups and interlibrary microfilm loan. Also worth checking **Chronicling America** (Library of Congress, free — different holdings) and the **Buffalo County Historical Society**.

## ⚠️ Two honest notes

**1. The idea was good; the archive just isn't there.** A small-town weekly reporting *"James Gleeson left Tuesday for a visit to his old home in Ireland"* is exactly the kind of item that would name a **townland or parish** — the thing this project most wants from a US source. The reasoning holds. **The Mondovi Herald is where that item lives, and it is on microfilm, not online.** This moves from "search" to "archive request."

**2. Newspapers.com is NOT a dead end generally — only for Buffalo County.** It supplied all **three 1946 obituary printings** (*St. Cloud Times*; *Leader-Telegram* ×2), because St. Cloud and Eau Claire are both well covered. The gap is specific and now documented.
