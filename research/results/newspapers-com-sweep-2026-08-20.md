## ⚠️ Two honest notes

**0. ⭐⭐ NEW THREAD — read the 19 pages.** `Gleeson Mondovi`, Eau Claire *Leader-Telegram*, **1905–1915**, is 19 pages. Somewhere in the Mondovi column for **December 1908** and **February 1909** there may be a line like *"James Gleeson left Tuesday for a visit to his old home in Ireland"* — the kind of notice that names a **townland or parish**. This is now a finite, tractable read rather than an archive request. **Do this before writing any letters.**

**0a. ⭐ Bina's maiden name may be LOUGHNEY.** The 5 Dec 1931 item calls **Bridget Loughney, 80** the mother of **"Mrs. James Gleeson."** In Dec 1931 that is **Sabina "Bina" Gleeson**, James's second wife (m. Jan 1928/29, d. 2 Aug 1937), whom both obituaries call *"Mrs. Bina O'Brien of Duluth"* — i.e. **O'Brien was a married name and she was a widow.** The 1930 census household (from the January tree print) reads: *James Gleason 64 · Vina Gleason 45 · John Gleason 31 · **Bridget Laughney 78** · Henry Weiss 21* — so Bridget was living with them. ⚠️ **Probable, not proven: Sabina was born LOUGHNEY.** Test with her 1937 death record or the 1928/29 marriage record.

**1. The idea was good; the archive just isn't there.** A small-town weekly reporting *"James Gleeson left Tuesday for a visit to his old home in Ireland"* is exactly the kind of item that would name a **townland or parish** — the thing this project most wants from a US source. The reasoning holds. **The Mondovi Herald is where that item lives, and it is on microfilm, not online.** This moves from "search" to "archive request."

**2. Newspapers.com is NOT a dead end generally — only for Buffalo County.** It supplied all **three 1946 obituary printings** (*St. Cloud Times*; *Leader-Telegram* ×2), because St. Cloud and Eau Claire are both well covered. The gap is specific and now documented.
