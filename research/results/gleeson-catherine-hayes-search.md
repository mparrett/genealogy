# Catherine Hayes née Gleason (Staten Island) — Identification + Open Irish-Side Question

**Search date:** 2026-06-03
**Triggering lead:** `assets/external/misc/ancestry-catherine-hayes-ny-death.md` — Ancestry-sourced NY death record names Catherine Hayes, b. Ireland 14 Feb 1876, d. 1 Nov 1944 Staten Island, parents Patrick Gleason + Margaret Gleason, "2nd great-grandaunt" tree marker.

## Resolution: Catherine identifies as "Mrs. John Hayes of Staten Island" from James's 1946 obit

The death record + tree marker close the long-standing identification gap left by the Ella Isabel Gleason withdrawal (2026-06-01). Catherine Hayes née Gleason **is** the third surviving sister James named in his 28 Jan 1946 St. Cloud Times obituary. Helen Ryan, the cousin who informed the death cert + served as executor, ties the NY-side and Irish-side family chains.

## US-side anchors (high confidence)

From the Ancestry death record + Find A Grave headstone (memorial 77836976, St Mary's Cemetery Grasmere, Staten Island, Reid Avenue Section):

- **Catherine Hayes** (née Gleason): b. ~1865 (per headstone) or 14 Feb 1876 (per death cert) – d. 1 Nov 1944, age 68 per cert / 79 per headstone. Address at death: **64 Edison St, Richmond Co., NY**. Widowed. Buried in shared RYAN family plot.
- **John Hayes** (husband): b. 1862 – d. 1929. Predeceased Catherine by 15 yrs.
- **Nora Gleason**: b. 1881 – d. 1939. **Buried in same plot.** Almost certainly Catherine's sister. **Strong working hypothesis:** Nora = Bridget Gleeson #2 (b. 11 Oct 1881 Croom; see facts.md), who emigrated to join Catherine in NY, took the name Nora in US life. The 1881 birth-year alignment is exact; Bridget #2 disappears from Irish records after 1881, consistent with emigration.
- **Thomas J. Ryan** (1899–1982) + **Helen J. Ryan** (1905–1989): same plot. Helen = informant + executor on Catherine's 1944 death cert (listed as Cousin).
- Spelling on the headstone is "**GLEASON**" — anglicized variant. The Croom-side family used Gleeson; the NY-emigrant branch drifted to Gleason. Same misspelling on Catherine's death-cert annotation.

## What this means

The combination of (a) confirmed US-side identification as Patrick + Margaret's daughter and (b) absent Irish-side baptism + civil-birth records points to one of:

1. **Catherine was born outside Croom parish.** Most plausibly in **Margaret's natal parish (likely Kilfinny, per 1918 Molony of Ballymackeamore deed lead)** — but possibly Tipperary or Cork per Robert's lore. Either way, sweep of those parish registers + civil-birth searches in adjacent SRDs is the next step. The 1894 arrival's Nenagh residence makes Tipperary baptisms worth checking.
2. **Civil registration missed her.** Mandatory from 1864, but rural Ireland 1864–1875 had imperfect compliance. The baptismal record (if a non-Croom parish) is then the only proof.
3. **The US records have wrong parent attribution.** Helen Ryan, the cousin informant, may have approximated. But the obit alignment ("Mrs. John Hayes") + Hayes/Gleason burial cluster + tree marker make this unlikely.
