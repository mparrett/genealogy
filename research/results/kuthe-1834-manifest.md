## Significance

- Confirms **Andreas Kuthe** (b. c. 1798) as a real person, not just family tradition
- Shows Frederick arrived as a **child** in 1834 with his family, not as an adult in 1845

- Origin listed as **Germany** (user notes Darmstadt; existing bio says Vöhl, Waldeck — needs reconciliation)

## Conflicts with Existing Research

### 1845 Olbers Manifest
The Frederick Kuthe bio currently states he emigrated in June 1845 on the ship *Olbers* from Bremen to New Orleans, listing an 18-year-old "Friederich" from Vöhl. If Frederick arrived in 1834 as a child, the 1845 Olbers entry may be:
- A different Kuthe/Küthe from Vöhl (not our Frederick)
- A return trip or second immigration (less likely)
- Incorrectly attributed to our line

**The Olbers data point needs scrutiny.**

### Origin: Darmstadt vs. Vöhl
- This manifest: Darmstadt (per user's source)
- Existing bio: Vöhl, Waldeck region of Hesse
- Both are in Hesse but ~150 km apart
- The Olbers manifest specifically says Vöhl — if that's a different family, the Vöhl connection may be wrong

### Birth Year
- Existing bio: c. 1822
- This manifest: age 7–10 in 1834, so born c. 1824–1827
- 1880 census says "about 57" → c. 1823
- Death in 1900 "at approximately 77" → c. 1823
- Range of c. 1822–1827 is plausible given imprecise 19th-century ages
