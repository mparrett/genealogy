# ⭐⭐ James J. Gleeson went BACK to Ireland in 1908–09

**Date:** 2026-08-20
**How it surfaced:** Matt said the obituary and marriage images were "in my Ancestry album, possibly Bear, definitely somewhere." Looking for those, I opened a file already sitting in this repo — `assets/external/ancestry/James Joseph Gleeson-ancestry-2026-01-26.pdf`, a print of his Ancestry profile from **26 January 2026**. The obituary images were not in it. Something better was.

---

## 1. The find

The January print lists his Arrival events. Among them:

> **Age 44 — Arrival · 16 Dec 1908 · Liverpool, England and Queenstown, Ireland**
> **Age 45 — Arrival · 14 Feb 1909 · New York, New York, USA**

**He returned to Ireland.** Outbound December 1908, home February 1909. He was 45. His father had been dead eleven years; **his mother Margaret was alive and living on Main Street, Croom** (she appears there in the 1901 and 1911 censuses, both of which we hold, and died in 1922).

⚠️ I had seen a "Queenstown → NY 1909" row in an earlier Ancestry search this session and **dismissed it as noise.** It was not noise.

### ⭐ Why this matters more than the 1883 record

The 1883 arrival is a **bare name + age + nationality**. This one carries **birth county (Limerick)**, **US residence (Mondovi)**, **wife's name (Maria)**, occupation, and citizenship — four independent hooks that all match our documented James J. Gleeson of Mondovi, husband of Maria Ann Fitzgerald.

**This is a documented identification.** The 1883 one is not. If we only get to keep one, keep this.

**It also gives us the first physical description of him we have.**

## 3. What the image adds, and what it refuses to give

Read directly at high zoom (claude-in-chrome screenshots of its own tab — the reliable route for Ancestry's canvas viewer).

**Page 1** (image 0671) is the standard 1907 form, headed *"LIST OR MANIFEST OF ALIEN PASSENGERS"* with **"Citizen"** written in above *"Alien"*. Line **10**: *Gleeson · James · 43 · m m · Farmer · ✓ ✓ · [stamp] · Mondovi · … · Wis.*

**⚠️ The "nearest relative or friend in country whence alien came" column exists on this form — and for his line it is struck through, blank.** That column is not filled for returning US citizens. So the one field that could have named someone in Croom is empty here too. (This is now the *second* time this session I have gone after that column and come away with nothing; the first time it did not exist at all.)

**Page 2** (image 0672) is the continuation sheet. Most of it is blank — cabin passengers largely skipped it — but **line 10 is one of only two filled rows on the page.** It carries:

- *Whether going to join a relative or friend* → **"Wife, Maria, Mandovi"**
- A long entry reading approximately **"Temporarily to visit … Mrs Ma[…] … Good — 1910 5th [Ave?] … / …d Court, Delaware Co., Iowa / 2 Nov 1896"**
- *Place of birth: Country / City or Town* → **"Ireland / Lim[erick]"**

### ⚠️⚠️ Two things on page 2 I could NOT resolve, and am not going to guess at

**(a) The "whether ever before in the United States — year" cell is FILLED.** This is potentially the single most valuable cell in the document: it is his own answer, in 1909, to when he first came. I can make out **two four-digit years, one beginning "188…" and one beginning "189…"**, with **1907** and **1908** written in the adjacent column, and what may be **"Mo"** and **"La."** beneath them.

**I cannot read the final digits at the resolution Ancestry's viewer delivers.** The first could be 1882, 1887 or 1889. I am not reporting a year off it. ⚠️ Note the temptation: **1882** would corroborate the 1920 census and would be a satisfying result — which is exactly why I should not squint it into existence. This is the same motivated-reading failure as "Maria Molony 1865."

**(b) "…d Court, Delaware Co., Iowa · 2 Nov 1896"** looks like a **court and a date** — the shape of a **naturalization**. If that reading holds, it names the court that naturalized him and the date, which is **Tier-1 thread #1 answered off a manifest.** But *Delaware County, Iowa* does not fit a man documented in Buffalo County, Wisconsin, and the entry may belong to the neighbouring line rather than his. **Unverified. Do not cite.**

### How to resolve both

Higher-resolution scans of this exact film exist elsewhere:
- **FamilySearch** holds NARA **T715** free.
- The **Statue of Liberty–Ellis Island Foundation** site carries high-resolution Lusitania manifests for this period.

Either should settle the year cell and the court entry. **That is now the highest-value cheap task on the board.**

## 4. What this does to the emigration question

We now have **four** competing answers, none of them agreeing:

| Source | Year |
|---|---|
| 1900 census (nearest to the event, internally corroborated by "years in US = 15") | **1885** |
| 1920 census | **1882** |
| The tree's manifest attachment | 1883 |
| **The 1909 manifest, in his own words** | **UNREAD — "188…"** |

**The 1909 answer is the best of the four** — given by the man himself, under oath, to an immigration officer, and closer to the event than the 1920 census. Reading that cell should be the next move on this thread.

## 5. ⚠️ What the January print says about the tree's immigration data

Matt's calibration — *"immigration and naturalization being exceptions"* — is confirmed exactly, and more sharply than he put it.

The January profile carries **seven separate Arrival events**:

> 1882 · **29 Sep 1883** · 1883 · 1885 · 1886 (Port of New York) · 1886 · and the 1908/1909 return pair.

These are not a conclusion. They are **every candidate he found, all attached at once** — a research net, correctly cast. His own note on the profile reads:

> **"Immigrated ~1883 when ~20??"**

— with the question marks. **He flagged the uncertainty himself, in January.** I then took one of the seven, treated it as settled, and demoted a Tier-1 thread on the strength of it. That correction already stands in `ancestry-tree-review-2026-08-20.md` §2a; this is the evidence behind it.

### Other January-print items worth noting

- **Parents shown as "Patrick Gleason 1843–1925" and "Margaritte Malony 1840–1886, d. Drouin, Victoria, AUSTRALIA."** The current tree shows 1829–1915 and 1836–1911. **Both versions conflict with our certificates** (Patrick d. 7 Dec 1897; Margaret d. 18 May 1922), and the Australian death is plainly a wrong-person attachment. The tree has changed a great deal since January, so a reconciliation must be done against the *live* tree, not this print.
- **"Age 39 — Birth of son Harold Francis Gleason, 4 Aug 1903 · Chicago, Cook, Illinois"** sits thirteen days from **"Anne Barbara Gleeson, 17 Aug 1903 · Mondovi."** Two sons in two states a fortnight apart is impossible; one is a wrong attachment. ⚠️ This is also the *actual* source of the "Chicago" thread I flagged earlier — not the 1883 ship.
- Source list includes **four separate** *Newspapers.com™ Obituary Index* entries, plus *UK and Ireland, Incoming Passenger Lists, 1878–1960* — the latter is the **outbound Dec 1908 leg**, and a UK-side list may record his **destination address in Ireland**. New thread.
