# Bridget Gleeson — Search for a Baptism (Hospital and Emly)

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Objective:** Find a baptism for Bridget Gleeson, daughter of James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien, to convert "probably Patrick's sister" from inference into documented fact.
**Result:** **Not found in either parish, and the search should now stop.** Hospital was read at 2× from January 1836 to December 1844; Emly was read for April 1831 – February 1833 and January 1834 – March 1835. Neither contains a baptism for a Bridget Gleeson to James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien. Three previously undocumented siblings were found along the way, plus evidence that materially *weakens* the sister hypothesis. See §8 and §10 for the conclusion.

Three points worth acting on:

- **Three previously undocumented siblings**: Mary (1830), Malachy (1835), James (1845). Malachy is a distinctive name and a good handle for future searches — a Malachy Gleeson with wife Biddy Dineen is raising children at Hospital from 1841, which is likely this Malachy or a namesake relative.
- **Margaret's baptism date conflicts.** The index says **5 June 1842**; `facts.md` says ~3 Nov/Dec 1842 at Herbertstown. One is wrong. This matters because Margaret's date is what constrains where a Bridget could fit.
- **Patrick's 1833 baptism is indexed at Emly, County Tipperary**, not Hospital. `facts.md` cites Hospital page 94, Ballinvriana townland. Emly is the adjacent parish across the county line, and the family evidently used both. Worth reconciling.

**The gap: no child recorded between July 1835 and June 1842.** Nearly seven years. That is precisely where a Bridget born c. 1838–41 would sit.

### What this adds

**The sponsorship is reciprocal.** Bridget Gleeson stood godmother to Patrick's firstborn Catherine in January 1862. Three years later, at Bridget's own firstborn, the godmother is **Margarita Gleeson**. The following year Thomas Shea and Ellen Moloney stand for Patrick's daughter Honoria. Three exchanges in four years, alternating between the two households.

**Who is Margarita Gleeson?** Two candidates, and both help:
1. **Margaret Moloney Gleeson**, Patrick's wife — i.e. Bridget's sister-in-law.
2. **Margaret Gleeson**, Bridget's own sister — the Margaret baptized at Hospital in 1842, who would be about 23 in 1865. Note that a **Margaret Gleeson married Patrick O'Dea at Croom on 4 August 1869**, with **Patrick Shanahan** as a witness. If that is the same woman, then *two* Gleeson sisters had migrated from Hospital to Croom.

A **Margarita Gleeson** also sponsors an unrelated Croom baptism on 27 March 1866 (Maria, illegitimate daughter of Michael Riordan and Ellen Sullivan), so she was locally active in her own right.

**The Storan thread runs through everything.** William Storan witnesses Bridget's 1864 marriage; Margarita Storan sponsors in 1866; Catherina Storan sponsors in 1870. And in March 1867 the Croom register married **Thaddaeus (Timothy) Gleeson to Ellen Storin**. Storan/Storin is a family closely bound to this Gleeson circle and is worth its own trace.

**The Moloney thread likewise.** John Moloney (1862 sponsor), Ellen Moloney (1864 witness, 1866 sponsor), Alicia Moloney (1873 sponsor), Thomas Moloney (1875 marriage witness alongside Margarita Gleeson).

## 4. One piece of evidence that cuts the other way

Thomas O'Shea and Bridget Gleeson named their daughters, in order: **Mary, Bridget, Jane, Catherine, Margaret**. Under the Irish naming convention the first daughter is usually named for the maternal grandmother. If Bridget were the daughter of James Gleeson and **Honora** O'Brien, a Honora would be expected early — and there is none.

This should be stated rather than buried. Three things soften it without dissolving it:

- The convention was followed loosely, and the first daughter may have been named for Thomas's mother instead.
- There is an unexplained gap between Brigida (1866) and Janeta (1870) into which an unlocated child fits.
- The 1875 Margarita has a **date conflict** — baptism indexed 26 March 1875 but civil birth 15 May 1875, which cannot both be right and may indicate two children conflated, or a further child.

Reading the 1875 register entry and searching Croom civil births for O'Shea children 1866–1870 would settle whether a Honora O'Shea existed.

Not documented.** Today's work did not find the record that would document it, and turned up one naming-pattern observation that argues mildly against.

## 6. Follow-up verified at image level, same session

### Margaret's 1842 baptism — date corrected, and it tightens the Bridget window

**Source:** NLI Hospital microfilm 02507/04 (**vtls000632731**), frame **6**, right page, under the heading *June 1842*.

> **5 — Marg[are]t — James Gleeson — Honora O'Brien — sp. Mich[ae]l Callahan, Marg[are]t O'Brien — Herbertstown**

- **Date: 5 June 1842.** The FamilySearch index is right and `facts.md` is wrong — the "~3 Nov/Dec 1842" should be corrected.
- **Sponsors and townland confirm `facts.md`:** Michael Callahan and Margaret O'Brien, at **Herbertstown**. The Callaghan kin-network reasoning in `facts.md` stands.

**Why this matters for Bridget.** A full sibling cannot be born to the same mother between roughly **September 1841 and March 1843**. Bridget's stated age of 22 in August 1864 implies birth between August 1841 and August 1842 — a window Margaret almost entirely occupies. So either Bridget was not a full sibling, or **her stated age is wrong**, which is the ordinary situation on Irish marriage certificates.

**Revised search window: 1838 to mid-1841** — squarely inside the documented seven-year gap — with a secondary window of **mid-1843 to 1844**. In frames:

### Croom frame 114 — the indexed 1875 O'Shea baptism is not where the index says

FamilySearch indexes a *Margarita O'Shea, baptized 26 March 1875, Croom, to Thomæ O'Shea and Brigidæ Gleeson*. Frame 114 (Jan–Jul 1875) was read: the entry at **26 March 1875 is a Collopy** — *Margarita filia Thomæ Collopy, spons. Edwardo Collopy et Mariâ Collopy* — and there is no O'Shea baptism at that date. The March section runs Die 5, 7, 7, 25 only.

The civil birth for Margaret O'Shea is **15 May 1875**, which does not match either. **Treat the indexed 26 March 1875 O'Shea baptism as unverified** — the surname is very likely an index error against the Collopy entry, and the real baptism should be sought around 16–17 May 1875 on frame 114 or 115.

## 7. The image sweep — first pass

**Register:** NLI Hospital microfilm 02507/03, **vtls000632730**, baptism frames.
**Method:** each page read in four horizontal bands at roughly 2× native, with grayscale/contrast enhancement applied in-canvas. The register's format is a running line per baptism — `[day] [child] [father] [mother] sp [sponsors] [townland]` — with no surname column, so every line has to be read rather than scanned.

### Result: one new sibling, and a clean stretch

**NEW — Edmund Gleeson, April 1838** (frame 181, right page, under *April 1838*):

> **Ed[mun]d — J[a]s Gleeson — Honora Brien — sp J[no?] Gleeson, Bidd[y ...]**

The child's forename reads **Edmund**, with *Edward* as a possible alternate; the day of the month falls in the gutter shadow and is not legible. The male sponsor's forename is uncertain — most likely **John Gleeson**, which together with the following "Biddy" would make the sponsors **John Gleeson and Biddy Higgins**, a documented Hospital couple raising children from 1836 to 1846. That reading, if right, strengthens `facts.md`'s hypothesis that John Gleeson was close kin.

**This is the eighth child now documented for James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien, and it lands squarely in what was previously a seven-year gap.**

**PROBABLE — Honora Gleeson, 20 September 1839** (frame 186, left page, under *September 1839*):

> **20 — Honor — J[a?o?]s Gleeson — Norry Brien — sp Tom Lonergan, Mary [Lacy] — Herbertstown**

The surname **Gleeson** and the mother **Norry Brien** are unambiguous, and *Norry* is the standard diminutive of Honora. The townland is **Herbertstown**, the same townland recorded for Margaret in 1842. **The father's forename cannot be resolved**: it is a two- or three-letter abbreviation with a superscript, and at the NLI scan's 1500 px ceiling the letterforms do not separate *Jas* from *Jos* even at maximum magnification. FamilySearch indexes it as "Jos".

- If **Jas** — this is a ninth child, **Honora**, born to James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien.
- If **Jos** — it belongs to a Joseph Gleeson. A Joseph Gleeson does appear at Hospital, but indexed with a wife **Mary Slattery**, not Norry O'Brien.

On the balance of the mother's name and the townland, this is **probably ours**, but the reading should not be treated as settled. A higher-resolution copy of frame 186 — a Family History Center scan, or Ancestry's or FindMyPast's imaging of the same film — would settle it.

## 8. The sweep is finished, and Bridget is not in it

**The Hospital baptism register has now been read at 2× from January 1836 to December 1844**, except for September 1841 – March 1843, which Margaret's June 1842 birth excludes biologically. Across those eight years there is **no baptism of a Bridget Gleeson to James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien.**

Method validation across the whole sweep: reading blind, the passes independently recovered **seven** baptisms that the FamilySearch index also lists — Jno Gleeson to Jno Gleeson & Biddy Higgins (Apr 1836), Mary to Michl Gleeson & Ellen O'Brien (Feb 1836), Mary to Js Gleeson & Kitty Green (Jun 1836), Wm to James Gleeson & Mary Slattery (Mar 1837), Kitty to Patt Gleeson & Margt Quirke (Jul 1839), Biddy to John Gleeson & Biddy Higgins (Aug 1840), and Matt to John Gleeson & Kate Brien (Jul 1844) — plus the 12 February 1839 entry the low-magnification pass had missed. The negatives are as controlled as this scan resolution allows.

### ⚠ How many James Gleesons were there in Hospital parish?

The sweep turned up **at least four distinct James Gleeson couples** in this one parish across nine years:

| Father | Wife | Attested |
|---|---|---|
| James Gleeson | **Honora O'Brien** | ours — Patrick 1829, Mary 1830, Malachy 1835, Edmund 1838, Honora? 1839, Margaret 1842, James 1845, Catherine 1850 |
| Js Gleeson | **Kitty Green** | Mary, June 1836 |
| James Gleeson | **Mary Slattery** | Wm, 14 March 1837 |
| Jas Gleeson | **Ellen Moore** | Norry, 11 November 1844, Herbertstown |

Some of these may be one man remarrying, but they cannot all be. **"James Gleeson" was a common name in Hospital parish, and at least one other James Gleeson lived at Herbertstown itself.**

### What this does to the sister hypothesis

The 1864 marriage certificate gives Bridget's father as "James Gleeson, farmer" and no more. When we found it, that read as a strong match to Patrick's father. It now reads as a **weak** one — the name is common in exactly the parish we were matching against, and the corroborating baptism that would have made it specific does not exist in the register.

What still stands, and stands well:

- Bridget was living at **Scagh** in August 1864 — Patrick's own townland.
- She was **godmother to Patrick's firstborn** in January 1862.
- Her husband **Thomas O'Shea godfathered Patrick's daughter Honoria** in March 1866, with Ellen Moloney at both events.

What no longer stands as it did:

- The father's-name match is now **weak**, not strong.
- The naming pattern of her own daughters — Mary, Bridget, Jane, Catherine, Margaret, **no Honora** — was always a mild counter-signal, and with the baptism absent it carries more weight than before.

**Revised reading: Bridget Gleeson was a Gleeson kinswoman of Patrick's, resident in his townland and embedded in his family's sponsorship circle — the relationship is real but its degree is unestablished. "Sister" is now a weaker hypothesis than it appeared after the certificate, not a stronger one.** The identification of the 1862 sponsor with the 1864 bride is unaffected and remains probable, bordering on established, since it rests on residence and associates rather than on the father's name.

### Where to look next, if this is worth pursuing

1. **Emly, Co. Tipperary** — Patrick's own 1833 baptism indexes there, not at Hospital, so the family demonstrably used both registers. Emly is the first place to search for a Bridget.
2. Neighbouring **Cashel & Emly** parishes more broadly.
3. **1830–1835 at Hospital** — the gaps between Mary (Aug 1830), Patrick (Mar 1833) and Malachy (Jul 1835). A Bridget there would be 29–34 at her 1864 marriage against a stated 22. A stretch, but the certificate age is the weakest fact in the chain.
4. **Bridget's death record**, if she died in Ireland — it would give an age and an informant, and the informant may be a relative worth identifying.

### Method validation

The 2× pass independently recovered four baptisms that the FamilySearch index also lists, without reference to the index while reading — *Biddy Gleeson to John Gleeson and Biddy Higgins, 3 August 1840* (frame 189), *Kitty Gleeson to Patt Gleeson and Margt Quirke, July 1839* (frame 185), *a child of Malachy Gleeson and Biddy Dineen, May 1841* (frame 191), and **the 12 February 1839 Gleeson × Mary Slattery baptism on frame 184 that the 1.05× pass had missed**. That last one is the direct demonstration that the re-read was necessary and that the depth is now sufficient.

### A caution on reading this hand

At scan depth the abbreviation **"Illeg^te"** (illegitimate), which this priest writes frequently in the child-name position, is easily mistaken for a surname — it cost me a false "Gleeson" on the first pass through frame 186. Frame 184's closing entry makes the usage explicit: *"Mary, Illeg^te, J[?] Gordan & Mary McKnight … 2nd Bastard by this Vagabond."* Anyone continuing this sweep should expect it.

**A caution on the four scan-depth frames.** They were read at about 1.05× native, and at that magnification the sweep is not reliable — the FamilySearch index lists a Gleeson baptism on 12 February 1839 that I did not pick up on frame 184. Frames 181–184 should be re-read at 2× before their negatives are treated as controlled. The three frames marked "full" were read at 2×, and that depth demonstrably works: it independently recovered the indexed *Biddy Gleeson, 3 August 1840, to John Gleeson and Biddy Higgins* on frame 189.

## 9. Emly parish swept — also clean

Because Patrick's 1833 baptism is in the **Emly** register (see §8), Emly was the obvious place for a Bridget who is not at Hospital. It has now been searched.

### Gleesons were rare at Emly

FamilySearch indexes only **four** Gleeson baptisms at Emly across the whole of 1820–1845:

| Year | Child | Parents |
|---|---|---|
| 1821 | Thos | Denis Gleeson & Mary D— |
| 1826 | Jno | Denis Gleeson & Mary D— |
| 1827 | Mary | Jno Gleeson & Julian Bourke |
| **1833** | **Patrick** | **James Gleeson & Honora O'Brien** — ours |

So our family's 1833 appearance is an **outlier in that register, not a pattern**. They were Hospital-parish people who used Emly once. The likely reason is visible on every page: **Ballinvriana**, their townland in 1833, recurs constantly as an Emly townland. They were living at the edge of the Emly catchment, moved to **Herbertstown** by 1838, and their register use follows the move.

### Frames read at image level

The Emly hand is far larger and cleaner than Hospital's — legible at full-page width, so no half-page crops were needed.

| Frames | Period | Result |

| **66–74** | **Apr 1831 – Feb 1833** | no Gleeson baptism to James & Honora |

These are the two windows adjacent to the family's documented Emly use — the gap between Mary (Aug 1830, Hospital) and Patrick (Mar 1833, Emly), and the gap between Patrick and Malachy (Jul 1835, Hospital). **Neither contains a Bridget, or any other child of James and Honora.**

### Incidental

- **Denis Gleeson** and **Mary Gleeson** appear as sponsors in 1831 — the Denis Gleeson household from the index above.

## 10. Recommendation: stop searching for Bridget's baptism

Between Hospital and Emly, the following is now read at image level with no Bridget Gleeson to James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien:

- **Hospital, Jan 1836 – Dec 1844** (Sep 1841 – Mar 1843 excluded biologically)
- **Emly, Apr 1831 – Feb 1833 and Jan 1834 – Mar 1835**

That covers every gap in the documented sibling sequence except **1830–31**, **1835**, and **1836–39 at Emly** — and a Bridget in any of those would be 25–34 at her 1864 marriage against a stated 22.

**The honest conclusion is that Bridget was probably not a daughter of James Gleeson and Honora O'Brien.** She was a Gleeson, she lived at **Scagh** in Patrick's own townland, she was godmother to his firstborn, and her husband godfathered his next daughter. That relationship is real and well evidenced. Its *degree* is not, and two parishes of negative evidence now argue against the simplest reading.

**Record her as: a Gleeson kinswoman of Patrick's, of unestablished degree.** Further baptism searching has poor expected return; if the degree matters, better routes are her **death record** (age and informant), or the **1901/1911 census** for surviving O'Shea children whose stated ages would bracket her birth.
