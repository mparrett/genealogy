# Croom Parish Marriage Register Review

**Source:** NLI Catholic Parish Registers, Microfilm vtls000634977 (registers.nli.ie)
**Reviewed:** 2026-04-11
**Pages reviewed:** 37 images covering 1844–45, 1849, 1853–1868, 1874
**Looking for:** Gleeson and Moloney/Maloney entries, especially Patrick Gleeson + Margaret Moloney marriage

## Key Finding: Bridget Gleeson + Maria Malony (August 1864)

**Page 153, right side, entry No. 27:**

> Thomam [McNamara?] et **Brigidam Gleeson** — coram ... **Maria Malony** — Joanis Dundon

A Bridget Gleeson married in Croom parish with Maria Maloney as a witness. This is NOT Patrick's daughter Bridget (born February 1868) — this Bridget may be Patrick's sister or another relative. The appearance of both Gleeson and Moloney surnames in the same marriage entry confirms the families were in each other's social network in Croom during the 1860s.

## Patrick Gleeson + Margaret Moloney Marriage: Not Found

The target marriage was not located in this batch. Possible reasons:

1. **Page 151 is badly obscured** — a shadow/thumb covers much of the page, and it appears to span 1862–1863, exactly the period we'd expect the marriage
2. The marriage may have taken place in a **neighboring parish** (e.g., Kilfinane, Bruff, Athlacca)
3. The pages are not strictly chronological on the microfilm — some gaps may exist in coverage
4. The marriage may predate 1853 (earliest coverage in the 126+ page range)
