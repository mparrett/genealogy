# Wisconsin Historical Society searches — two real finds from Matt's links

**Date:** 2026-08-20 · Matt sent three links; this is what came of them.

## ⭐⭐ FIND 1 — the 1892 marriage is INDEXED, with an exact citation

**WHS Pre-1907 Vital Records index, Buffalo County:**

> **Gleason, James — Marriage Record — 23 November 1892 — Buffalo County**
> **Volume 2, Page 102 · Index Volume F · Sequence Number 03071**
> `wisconsinhistory.org/record/vital/MR1381044/gleason-james`

**Why this matters as much as the Catholic register.** Wisconsin marriage records of this period routinely record **each party's birthplace** and **the names of both sets of parents**. If the 1892 civil return does, it gives us:

- a **contemporary civil record naming where in Ireland he was born**, and
- an **independent American attestation of Patrick Gleeson and Margaret Moloney**.

That is the same payoff as the La Crosse diocesan ask, from a **second, independent source**. Either one landing would be a major result; both agreeing would be decisive.

⚠️ **We cannot see the full record online.** WHS states plainly: *"Wisconsin State Law prohibits the online reproduction of a complete vital record."* Three routes: view the microfilm in person at Madison, hire a researcher to print it, or order from the **Wisconsin State Vital Records Office**. **The exact citation above makes any of the three cheap and certain** — this is no longer "please search for Gleeson," it is "please copy Volume 2, page 102."

## ⭐ FIND 2 — LEO GLEASON, died 26 February 1905, Buffalo County

> **Gleason, Leo — Death Record — 26 February 1905 — Buffalo County**
> **Volume 1, Page 375 · Microfilm Reel 009 · Sequence Number 124724**
> `wisconsinhistory.org/record/vital/DR333441/gleason-leo`

**A strong candidate for one of the missing children.** Both 1946 obituaries say **twelve children** were born to James and Maria; we have documented nine or ten. A Leo Gleason dying in Buffalo County in **1905** — the years the family was at Mondovi — fits an infant or small child of theirs.

⚠️ **Unproven.** The abbreviated index shows no age and no parents. The full record would settle it, and it is one line away on the same order form as the marriage.

## Also in the Buffalo County index, unresolved

- **Gleason, Maria — Marriage — 27 May 1884**
- **Gleason, Mary Ann — Marriage — 12 May 1880**

Both **pre-date** James's arrival in Buffalo County and his 1892 marriage. Either other Gleasons were already in the county, or these are women marrying *out* of a Gleason family. ⚠️ Low priority, but they mean **"Gleason" in Buffalo County was not exclusively our family** — the same caution as Eau Claire.

**⚠️ Spelling note, useful for every future search:** `Gleeson` + Buffalo County returns **nothing** in the WHS index. The family is indexed there as **GLEASON**. Search both, always.
