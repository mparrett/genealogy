### What This Adds to the Picture

**Patrick Gleeson's line:** John Gleeson (Main Street, Croom, 1851, tenant of Croker) → Patrick Gleeson (born ~1837, Carrier/Farmer, died Dec 1897, Main Street Croom) → Mary and Bridget (1901–1911 census, Main Street Croom). The Gleeson family held the same property on Main Street across at least three generations.

**Margaret Moloney's line:** Margaret Moloney (Glenbevan, 1851, tenant of Joseph Bevan) is almost certainly the generation above our 1906 Margaret. The Moloneys were a substantial Croom family, some holding property as landlords on Bridge Street. The "Ballinscola" address in the will calendar is a small rural holding just outside Croom town.

**The link between them** is still the main open question — but the geography now makes it clear: both families were deeply embedded in Croom town and its immediate surroundings, within a mile of each other. The most likely scenario is that our Margaret Moloney married into or was born into a Moloney branch connected to the Gleeson family through the Croom town community. Ellen (the informant on Margaret's death cert) is the key bridge figure still to be identified.
