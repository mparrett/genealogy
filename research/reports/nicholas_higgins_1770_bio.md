# Nicholas Higgins (1770 – c. 1867)
**Relationship:** 5th great-grandfather (Higgins line)

*The Generation That Left Wicklow for Dublin*

Nicholas Higgins was born in 1770 in County Wicklow, the youngest known child of **John Higgins** (1718–1808) and **Anne Thomas** (1733–1773) of Baltyboys, in the parish of Blackditches/Boystown/Valleymount. He was three years old when his mother Anne died in June 1773.

By the early 1800s Nicholas had moved his branch of the family into Dublin, settling at an address on **Charlotte Street** on the south side of the city. His wife **Eleanora Smyth** (1769–1811) had been baptized at St. Paul's Dublin in November 1769, the daughter of Patrick Smith and Sarah. Together Nicholas and Eleanora raised at least eight children:

- **Anne** (b. 1790)
- **Michael** (1792–1864) — founded the Ballynatona branch documented by Tony Pearson's tree
- **Julia** (1796–1811)
- **John** (1797–1878) — died Co. Wicklow
- **Nicholas** (b. 1798)
- **Patrick** (1802–1802) — died in infancy
- **Bridget**
- **Christopher** (1803) — our line

Eleanora died on June 1, 1811, at the Charlotte Street house, when Christopher was seven years old. In the same year, Nicholas erected a tombstone at Baltyboys Cemetery in Valleymount for Eleanora, and three years earlier he had erected a second stone for his father John (d. September 24, 1808) and his mother Anne (d. June 24, 1773) — physically anchoring the family's connection to its Wicklow origins even as he raised the next generation in Dublin.

By 1825 Nicholas appears in the **Tithe Applotment Books at Rathgar / Rathfarnham**, a southern Dublin suburb — confirming that the family had become settled urban Dubliners by the time his son Christopher married Bridget Darcy back at Valleymount that August. Nicholas lived into his nineties, dying around 1867.

**Notes:**

- Birth: 1770, Co. Wicklow.
- Parents: John Higgins (1718–24 Sep 1808) + Anne Thomas (1733–24 Jun 1773), buried Baltyboys Cemetery, Valleymount.
- Wife: Eleanora Smyth (bapt. 30 Nov 1769, St. Paul's Dublin – 1 June 1811, Charlotte St., Dublin). Eleanora's parents: Patrick Smith + Sarah.
- Residence: Charlotte Street, Dublin (from at least 1808 erection of Baltyboys tombstone).
- 1825: appears in Tithe Applotment Books at Rathgar / Rathfarnham.
- Children: Anne (1790), Michael (1792–1864), Julia (1796–1811), John (1797–1878, d. Co. Wicklow), Nicholas (b. 1798), Patrick (1802–1802 infant), Bridget, Christopher (1803, our line).
- Death: c. 1867.
- Sources: Irish Catholic Parish Registers 1655–1915; Tithe Applotment Books; Baltyboys tombstone transcription and photograph; per Mary Anne Higgins (Australia-branch researcher), Mar 2022.
