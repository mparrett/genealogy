# John Higgins (1718 – 24 Sep 1808)
**Relationship:** 6th great-grandfather (Higgins line)

*Of Baltyboys, in the Wicklow Mountains*

John Higgins was born in 1718 in County Wicklow, in the upland district around Valleymount where the family had its ancestral seat. He died on **September 24, 1808**, at age ninety — an unusually long life for an Irishman of his generation — and was buried in **Baltyboys Cemetery** in the parish of Blackditches/Boystown/Valleymount, looking south across the Liffey valley toward the Wicklow Mountains.

He had married **Anne Thomas** (1733–1773), born in Dublin and likely brought into the family through a Dublin-to-Wicklow connection that the surviving record does not explain. Anne died young, on **June 24, 1773**, leaving John a widower for the last thirty-five years of his life. She was forty.

John and Anne's known children, traced through parish baptismal records and probate, were:

- **Laurence** (1754)
- **Andrew** (1759–1794) — died young, intestate probate filed
- **John** (1764–1837)
- **Catherine** (bapt. 1769, St. Nicholas, Dublin)
- **James** (1767) — likely the "James Higgins" of Ballynatona in the Tithe Applotment Books
- **Nicholas** (1770) — our line; would move to Dublin and raise eight children at Charlotte Street
- **Bridget** (bapt. 1778, St. Catherine's, Dublin)

The tombstone at Baltyboys, erected by John's son Nicholas in 1808 (and again for Eleanora in 1811), still stands and has been transcribed and photographed. It serves as the documentary anchor for the entire Higgins line — the rock-cut record that connects the Dublin-Newark-Arizona descendants of the twentieth century to the Wicklow farming family of the eighteenth.

- Birth: 1718, Co. Wicklow.

- Children: Laurence (1754), Andrew (1759–1794, intestate probate), John (1764–1837), James (1767, likely "James Higgins" of Ballynatona in Tithe Applotments), Catherine (bapt. 1769 St. Nicholas Dublin), Nicholas (1770, our line), Bridget (bapt. 1778 St. Catherine's Dublin).
