# Web Search: Tipperary Patrick Gleeson + Honora O'Brien — Naas 1888 collateral kin

**Date:** 2026-06-05

Looking for indexed Irish records to upgrade a Tipperary collateral-kin lead from T3 → T2 (or kill it). The lead, if it holds, also helps resolve a separate question about which of two same-parents baptisms is my direct ancestor.

## The lead

A Catholic marriage register at **Naas, Co. Kildare, 1888** records:

- **Groom:** Michael Gleeson, residence Limerick
- **Bride:** Johanna Hayde, residence Naas
- **Groom's parents:** **Patk Gleeson & Honora O'Brien**, residence **Tipperary**
- **Bride's father:** John Hayde, Naas
- **Note:** "Disp. by V.G." (dispensed by Vicar General)

(Source: Naas parish marriage register 1888, row 9 on the page; transcript on file.)

A separate hit in the **1918 Calendar of Wills, page 208** records the only "Patrick Gleeson + Honora Gleeson widow" couple I've found in Tipperary in that window:

> **Gleeson, Patrick.** Munnia, Killoscully, Newport, Co. Tipperary, **Labourer**. Limerick D.R. Will. Grant – 11 March, 1918. Execix. – Honora Gleeson, widow.

I suspect this Killoscully couple is the same Patrick + Honora named as Michael's parents in Naas 1888. If so, Michael was likely born in or near Killoscully (Newport, Co. Tipperary) c. 1858–1866.

## Why this matters to me (context, not for you to verify)

My direct ancestor Patrick Gleeson (b. ~1832, Hospital parish, Co. Limerick, m. Margaret Moloney, settled at Croom) had parents **James Gleeson + Honora O'Brien**. The Hospital register has two same-parents baptisms — a Patrick in 1829 and another Patrick in 1833 — and I don't yet know which is mine. If the Naas 1888 Michael is the son of a *surviving* 1829 Patrick (who would have married a different Honora O'Brien and stayed in Tipperary), then Michael is my James J. Gleeson (b. 1863)'s first cousin, **and the 1833 baptism is mine**. The Killoscully/Newport area Patrick + Honora couple is the best candidate I've seen for a surviving 1829 Patrick.

## What I'd like to find

1. **Michael Gleeson's baptism record** in Tipperary parishes ~1858–1866, parents **Patrick Gleeson (Labourer) + Honora O'Brien**. 3. **The actual probate document for the Killoscully Patrick** (Limerick District Registry, will granted 11 March 1918, executrix Honora Gleeson widow). If accessible online — National Archives of Ireland calendars / Findmypast / Ancestry Ireland Will Calendars — would it name **Honora's maiden name** in the body of the will? ### Tier 2 — context
4. Other Killoscully / Munnia / Newport-area **Gleeson baptisms** ~1855–1880 — children of Patrick + Honora besides Michael (siblings of Michael).

Any indexed probate or census record naming a relationship between these three Newport Gleesons would be useful.
