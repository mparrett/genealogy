Looking for any indexed Irish/US records that would help decide which of two same-parents baptisms is my direct ancestor. Casting a wide net — happy to receive even tangential leads if they touch the people involved.

## The two candidate baptisms (both at Hospital parish, Co. Limerick, same parents)

- **Baptism A — 20 March 1829, Hospital townland.** Patrick filius James Gleeson + Honora O'Brien. Sponsors **Stephen Gleeson + Bridget Higgins**. (Source: NLI Hospital parish CPR.)
- **Baptism B — 17 March 1833, Ballinvriana townland.** Patrick (2nd) filius James Gleeson + Honora O'Brien. Sponsors **Pat O'Brien + Ellen Walsh**. (Source: NLI Hospital parish CPR.)

Both children almost certainly survived to adulthood (mother had at least 4 documented children 1829–1850, no infant-death entries surfaced). My direct ancestor "Patrick Gleeson, Croom shopkeeper" (b. ~1832 calibrated) is **one** of these two. The other Patrick may have survived elsewhere — most likely staying in the Hospital/Tipperary borderland.

## What I know about my Patrick (Baptism A or B; not yet determined)

- Moved into Croom village; occupation **Shopkeeper** by 11 Oct 1881 (civil birth registration of daughter Bridget, with Patrick as informant).
- Married **Margaret Moloney** (b. c. 1836 Croagh/Kilfinny parish per candidate baptism). Children baptized Croom 1863–1881.
- Died between 3 Nov 1881 and 31 Mar 1901 (Margaret recorded as widow on 1901 Census).
- His son **James Joseph Gleeson b. 23 Dec 1863 Croom** later emigrated to Wisconsin/Minnesota.
