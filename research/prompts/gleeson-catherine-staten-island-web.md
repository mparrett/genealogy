# Web Search: Catherine Hayes née Gleeson + Nora Gleason — Staten Island, NY

**Date:** 2026-06-05

Looking for indexed U.S. records to tighten the trail of an Irish immigrant woman who settled on Staten Island, and to test one specific sibling hypothesis. I have the Irish-side identity locked from parish records; the U.S.-side documentation is thinner and almost certainly hiding on the indexed public web.

## The people I'm looking into

- **Catherine Hayes née Gleeson** — born Croom, Co. Limerick, Ireland, ~1857–1862 (headstone gives 1865 but that's almost certainly an engraver/family approximation; Croom baptism not yet located). Daughter of **Patrick Gleeson (Shopkeeper, Croom)** + **Margaret Moloney**.
- **Her husband John Hayes** — Irish immigrant, predeceased her in **1929**. Likely born Ireland ~1855–1862; possibly Limerick or Cork. They met and married in Ireland before emigrating.
- **"Nora Gleason"** — buried in the *same* family plot at St Mary's Grasmere; I have her as **1881–1939** but haven't seen her death cert. **Hypothesis I'm testing:** Nora is actually Catherine's younger sister **Bridget Gleeson** (civil birth 11 Oct 1881, Croom), who emigrated post-1881 to join Catherine and went by "Nora" in U.S. life.

## What I know (high confidence, locked)

- **Catherine + John married 29 Nov 1879 at Croom Catholic Church, Co. Limerick** (NLI parish 0881, register vtls000634977, page 161). Officiant Rev. John Quinlan CC. Witnesses: **Michael Shanahan + Catharina Shanahan** (née Lynch).
- **Catherine died 1 Nov 1944** at **64 Edison Street, Staten Island, NY (Grant City neighborhood)**, widowed.
- **John Hayes died 1929** (NY).
- Both buried at **St Mary's Cemetery, Grasmere, Staten Island — Reid Ave Section — in the RYAN family plot**.
- Catherine = **"Mrs. John Hayes, Staten Island NY"** named as surviving sister in her brother James J. Gleeson's 1946 obituary (St. Cloud Times, MN).
- Catherine's NY death record lists parents as **Patrick + Margaret Gleason**, confirming the line.
- **Catherine had a sister Nora living separately on Staten Island:** Nora Gleason, d. 10 Feb 1939, NY death cert no. 317, single, housemaid, 232 Prall Ave Staten Island (Bloomfield/Travis area). Catherine signed as informant identifying herself as sister, naming parents Patrick Gleason + Margaret Maloney, Ireland. Nora arrived US ~1899 (40 yrs); buried St Mary's Cem. 13 Feb 1939 — almost certainly the same Ryan plot. Working ID: Nora = our Honora 1866 with ~10-yr age understatement on the cert. See [[gleeson-nora-1876-1939-sister]].
