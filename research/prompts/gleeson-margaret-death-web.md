# Web Search: Margaret Gleeson née Moloney — death record after Jan 1918, Ireland

**Date:** 2026-06-05

Small, focused search. Looking for one Irish civil death registration. I have the lower bound nailed and the geography tight, but the upper bound is open.

## The death I'm looking for

- **Name:** Margaret Gleeson, formerly Moloney (widow of Patrick Gleeson, Shopkeeper)
- **Date of death:** **after January 1918** (lower bound is hard — she signed a deed Jan 1918)
- **Place of death:** most likely **Croom, Co. Limerick** (she was living at Main Street, Croom from at least 1881 onward), with **Limerick city** or **Croagh/Kilfinny** (her natal area) as secondary possibilities
- **Age at death:** likely 82–95 (born c. 1836, range 1834–1838)
- **Marital status:** **widow** (Patrick died between 3 Nov 1881 and 31 Mar 1901)

## What I know (high confidence)

- Margaret was a literate, propertied widow in Croom — she ran the family shop on Main Street after Patrick's death and was still signing legal documents in January 1918.
- She was enumerated as a widow on the **1901 census** at Main Street, Croom, and again on the **1911 census** in Croom.
- Her surname is **Moloney** (one L). It appears as **Molony**, **Moloney**, and rarely **Maloney** in records; **Maloney** in particular is *usually* the wrong family.
- Her husband Patrick was **literate** and signed his name **"Patt"** — she was likely literate too.
- Her children's names (so likely informants on her death certificate): Catherine (in NY), James (in US/MN), Honora, Bridget, Ellen, Mary, Margaret, Jane, Bridget #2 (b. 1881).

## What I'd like to find

The **civil death registration** (Form A) for Margaret Gleeson née Moloney, 1918 or later, in **Croom Superintendent Registrar's District** (preferred), failing that **Rathkeale**, **Limerick (city)**, or **Newcastle West** SRDs. Specifically:

1. **Exact date and place of death.**
2. **Age at death** (would narrow her birth year — currently c. 1836 ±2).
3. **Cause of death.**
4. **Informant** — if a child signed as informant, that's gold (would identify which children were near her at the end).
5. **Burial location** (the headstone, if findable on Find-A-Grave, often gives age more accurately than the civil cert).

## Negative evidence I already have

- Margaret is **not in the 1858–1922 Calendar of Wills and Administrations** under any spelling. That's not evidence of a particular death year — many small estates didn't go to probate — but it does tell us she didn't leave a high-value or contested estate in those years.
- She is not on the 1911 census as having died between 1901 and 1911 (she's enumerated alive in 1911).

## Wrong-family records to reject

- **Margaret Mahony** (the *Australian* Patrick Gleeson's wife, d. 25 Oct 1886, Drouin, Victoria, Australia) — different woman entirely. Anything dating her death to 1886 in Australia is the Australian family.
- **Margaret Moloney** records in Co. Clare, Tipperary, or Cork without a Patrick Gleeson husband and Croom-shop residence — likely a different woman.
- Any **Margaret Gleason** death in the U.S. — she did not emigrate (she stayed in Croom and ran the shop).

## Sources to try

- **irishgenealogy.ie civil death index 1918–1958** — search Gleeson + variants (Gleason, Glissen), then filter for Croom/Rathkeale/Limerick SRDs. The civil-death index is fully indexed and image-linked through ~1973 for deaths.
- **Margaret + Patrick variants:** *Moloney, Molony, Maloney, Mullowney* — try all four spellings on the maiden name.
- **rootsireland.ie** Limerick database (paywall) — may have a Croom Catholic parish burial record if she was buried via the parish.
- **Find-A-Grave** — Croom (Manister) cemetery; Margaret may be buried with Patrick.
- **Newspapers** — *Limerick Leader*, *Munster News*, *Limerick Chronicle* obituary archives via the British Newspaper Archive / Findmypast Irish newspapers, 1918–1935.

For each find: **exact date, place, source, link, age at death verbatim, informant name verbatim**. For empty searches: a brief "checked X, no match" so I can rule it out cleanly.

The single most valuable thing you could return is **the death cert image with age at death** — that age, properly calibrated, would resolve her birth-year window and is the single best independent check on the *1836 Croagh baptism = our Margaret* hypothesis I'm currently working on.
