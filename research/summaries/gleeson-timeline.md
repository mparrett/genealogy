# Gleeson Line Timeline — Patrick (c. 1832) → James J. (1946)

**Purpose:** A clean chronological rail of dated events covering Patrick Gleeson (b. c. 1832 Co. Limerick) through his son James J. Gleeson (d. 1946 Waite Park MN). Useful for review after a long working session; cites the source for each row but skips narrative analysis.

**Last refreshed:** 2026-06-03

Conventions: ⭐ = high-confidence anchor; (probable) = strong inference; (TBD) = lead not yet pulled. Source short forms expanded at bottom.
