## Thread: Gleeson–Moloney 1860 marriage dispensation (Irish diocesan archives)

Related research: `research/outreach/gleeson-dispensation-inquiries-2026-08-20.md` · `research/findings/patrick-gleeson/facts.md` (18 Nov 1860 row)

**The ask in one line:** Patrick Gleeson and Margaret Moloney married in 1860 with a dispensation in the **3rd and 4th degree of consanguinity** — proof the Gleesons and Moloneys shared an ancestor c.1740–70. The Croagh registers begin only in 1836, so no parish record can show how. A surviving dispensation petition would state the relationship path and might name the common ancestor.

## Thread: James J. Gleeson's American years (Wisconsin archives)

Related research: `research/outreach/gleeson-wisconsin-archive-inquiries-2026-08-20.md` · `research/results/porters-mills-eau-claire-2026-08-20.md` · `research/results/gleeson-1909-return-voyage-2026-08-20.md`

**The ask in one line:** James J. Gleeson was born at Croom, Co. Limerick on 21 Dec 1863 and farmed at Mondovi, Buffalo Co., WI from 1892 to c.1941 — but **no American record made in his lifetime names a place in Ireland.** He **sailed home to Ireland 16 Dec 1908 (Cunard *Lucania* → Queenstown) and returned 14 Feb 1909 (*Lusitania*)**, and a local paper or a Catholic marriage register could name the parish or townland.
