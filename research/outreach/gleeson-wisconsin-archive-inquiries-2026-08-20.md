## ⚠️ Read this before sending anything

**Do the free online thing first.** The Eau Claire *Leader-Telegram*'s **Mondovi column** is on Newspapers.com, and `Gleeson Mondovi` for **1905–1915** is **only 19 pages**. If the Ireland-trip notice is in there, three of the four asks below become unnecessary. **Read those 19 pages, then send.**

These letters are still worth having drafted — a hometown weekly carries far more than a city paper's county column — but the sequencing matters.

## The ask, in one line

**James J. Gleeson** — born **21 December 1863 at Croom, Co. Limerick, Ireland**; emigrated as a young man (year uncertain, **1882–1885**); worked at **Porter's Mills** and **Eau Claire**; married **Maria Ann Fitzgerald, 23 November 1892, Buffalo County**; farmed near **Mondovi** until about 1941; died **28 January 1946** and is buried at **Sacred Heart Cemetery, Mondovi**. **We are trying to establish (a) when he arrived and (b) any American record that names a place in Ireland more precise than "Croom."**

**The single best target date pair:** he **sailed for Ireland from New York on 16 December 1908** (Cunard *Lucania*, landing Queenstown) and **returned to New York on 14 February 1909** (*Lusitania*) — a two-month visit home, documented at both ends. A local paper reporting that visit could name a townland or a relative.

| | |
|---|---|
| **To** | `library.archives@uwec.edu` · 715-836-2739 · McIntyre Library 5022, 103 Garfield Ave, Eau Claire, WI 54701 |
| **Why them first** | ⭐ **The Eau Claire ARC serves Buffalo County** — one of its six counties (Buffalo, Chippewa, Clark, Eau Claire, Rusk, Taylor). So it may hold **both** the Mondovi newspapers **and** the lumber records. They state they aim to fulfil research needs remotely and provide digitised copies. |
| **Hours** | Mon–Fri 9–4 |

**Four asks:**

1. **Buffalo County newspapers** — do they hold the *Mondovi Herald* (1890–1923) or *Buffalo County Herald* (1876–1890), and would they check four short windows: **late Nov–early Dec 1892** (marriage), **mid–late Dec 1908** and **Feb 1909** (departure and return from Ireland), and **late Jan–early Feb 1946** (death)?
2. **North Western Lumber Company Records, 1870–1955** (their own collection, 38 linear feet) — ⚠️ *the ArchivesSpace summary describes corporate, land and financial material and lists no payroll series.* So the ask is honest: **is there any employee-level material anywhere in it — time books, payrolls, wage ledgers, boarding-house or company-store registers — that the summary does not itemise?**
3. **Valley Lumber Company of Eau Claire** — any records at all. *(This is the firm named in the 1884 city directory entry we are trying to attribute.)*
4. **Porter's Mills / Porterville as a place** — company store ledgers, school or church records, photographs, plat maps, or a village history.

| | |
|---|---|
| **To** | "Ask Archives" / "Ask Library" web forms · Library 608-264-6535 · Archives 608-264-6460 · 816 State Street, Madison, WI 53706 |
| **Why** | WHS holds the master Wisconsin newspaper microfilm collection. If Eau Claire does not have the *Mondovi Herald*, Madison will. |

**Asks:** confirm holdings and reel numbers for the *Mondovi Herald* 1890–1923 and *Buffalo County Herald* 1876–1890; check the same four date windows; and confirm whether reels circulate on interlibrary loan.

⚠️ **Practical notes to set expectations:**
- Copy orders carry a **service charge plus 25¢/page** — **$20 + copies for out-of-state non-members**, $10 for members. Rush (7 working days) adds $25.
- Turnaround is **two to four weeks**.
- **Their Microfilm Reproductions Lab is not currently accepting duplication or digitisation requests** — so ask for *lookups and copies*, not for reels to be scanned.
- They ask for specific citations, so the letter gives exact dates rather than "please search for Gleeson."

## Draft 4 — ⭐ Diocese of La Crosse, Archives *(the one I nearly missed)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **To** | Genealogical Records Request web form at `diolc.org/archives/genealogical-records-request/` · 608-788-7700 · PO Box 4004, La Crosse, WI 54602-4004 |
| **Why** | Mondovi is in the **Diocese of La Crosse**. **Catholic marriage registers frequently record each party's place of birth** — and this couple married in a Catholic church in **November 1892**. |

**This is the highest-upside ask on the page.** If the Sacred Heart, Mondovi marriage register for 23 Nov 1892 names his birthplace, it could give a **parish or townland in Co. Limerick from a contemporary record**, written when he was 28 rather than reported by a Minnesota newspaper 54 years after his death. It could also name his parents — which would be an independent US-side attestation of Patrick Gleeson and Margaret Moloney.

**Also ask:** the 1928/29 marriage to **Sabina "Bina" O'Brien**, which may settle whether her maiden name was **Loughney**.

⚠️ **Expect delay.** Their own notice warns of a backlog and an absent archivist; responses were running to "late August or September." Ask by **web form**, not email. Note also that some parish registers stay **at the parish**, so a second, direct letter to Sacred Heart Parish, Mondovi may be needed.
